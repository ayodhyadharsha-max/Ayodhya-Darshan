Quick answers

Ayodhya tour package — your questions, answered

How to book VIP Darshan / Sugam Darshan at Ayodhya Ram Mandir?+
Official Sugam Darshan (VIP Passes) or Aarti passes can be booked online for free on the Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra portal. Alternatively, our comprehensive Ayodhya tour packages include complete VIP darshan booking assistance, ensuring a smooth, hassle-free yatra for families and senior citizens.
How much does an Ayodhya Dharshan tour package cost?+
An Ayodhya Dharshan tour package starts from ₹7,900 per person for a 3-day / 2-night trip, including stays, private travel, a guide and darshan assistance. The final price depends on hotel category, group size and season — larger groups pay less per person.
How many days do you need for Ayodhya darshan?+
2 nights / 3 days is ideal for an unhurried visit to the Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir, Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan and the Saryu aarti. Add 1–3 days to also cover Varanasi, Prayagraj or Chitrakoot.
How far is Varanasi from Ayodhya, and how many days are needed?+
Varanasi is about 200–230 km from Ayodhya — roughly 5–6 hours by road. A combined Ayodhya–Varanasi tour package is comfortable in 4 days / 3 nights, from ₹12,900 per person.
Which cities does Ayodhya Dharshan cover?+
We cover eight holy cities of Uttar Pradesh: Ayodhya, Varanasi (Kashi), Prayagraj, Chitrakoot, Naimisharanya, Vindhyachal, Mathura and Vrindavan — individually or as one Ramayana Circuit.
Can you arrange VIP or fast-track Ram Mandir darshan?+
Yes. Wherever the temple administration offers sugam / suvidha darshan, we arrange priority entry, aarti seating, and senior or wheelchair assistance so you spend your time in prayer, not in queues.
How do I book an Ayodhya or Varanasi tour package?+
Call or WhatsApp +91 92352 22399, email yatra@ayodhyadharshan.com, or fill the enquiry form on our Contact page. A yatra guide will send a tailored itinerary and quote — with no obligation.
