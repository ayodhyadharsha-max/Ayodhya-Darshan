2. Route Overview & Driving Conditions (NH27 Highway)

The journey from Gorakhpur to Ayodhya follows National Highway 27 (NH27), one of North India's smoothest 4-lane national express highways:

  • Route: Gorakhpur → Sahjanwa → Khalilabad → Basti Bypass → Harraiya → Ayodhya Dham (Ram Path).
  • Driving Duration: Approximately 2 hours 20 minutes to 2 hours 45 minutes under normal traffic conditions.
  • Highway Facilities: Excellent vegetarian restaurants, clean restrooms, and tea stalls are available along the Basti bypass corridor.
  • Door-to-Door Drop: Our drivers deliver you directly to your Ayodhya hotel or to the Sugam Darshan entry gates without needing to switch to autos.

3. Combine with Gorakhnath Temple & Nepal Border Circuit

Many yatris arriving in Eastern UP combine their pilgrimage across three revered shrines:

  • Gorakhnath Dham: Pay obeisance at the seat of Guru Gorakhnath in Gorakhpur before embarking for Ayodhya.
  • Maghar: Sacred samadhi and mazaar of Sant Kabir Das located just 25 km from Gorakhpur along NH27.
  • Janakpur Dham & Lumbini Excursion: Private cross-border cab services are also available for devotees extending their Ramayana pilgrimage to Janakpur (Mata Sita's birthplace).
