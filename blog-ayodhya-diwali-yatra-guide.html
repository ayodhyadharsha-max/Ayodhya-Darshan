Ayodhya Diwali Yatra Guide: Deepotsav & Ram Mandir Timings

Experiencing Diwali in Ayodhya—the land where the festival of lights originated—is a life-changing spiritual journey. The grand Ayodhya Deepotsav sees the city lit with more than 2.5 million clay lamps (diyas) along the Saryu River ghats and Ram Ki Paidi, creating a breathtaking celestial scene on Earth. In this guide, we cover everything you need to plan your Ayodhya Diwali yatra successfully.

Ram Mandir Diwali Darshan Timings

During the Diwali festival week, the Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir observes special worship rituals. Devotees should take note of the following darshan slots:

  • Morning Slot: 6:30 AM to 12:00 PM (Special Shringar Aarti at 6:30 AM requires pre-arranged passes).
  • Evening Slot: 2:00 PM to 9:30 PM (Grand Deepawali Sandhya Aarti at 7:30 PM).
  • Bhog/Break: The temple is closed for public entry between 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM for Bhog and rest.

Given the heavy rush, booking a custom VIP Darshan pass upgrade is highly recommended to bypass long public queues.

Diwali Travel Rules & Barricading Restrictions

Due to the massive crowds and VVIP movements during Deepotsav, Ayodhya administration enforces strict traffic regulations:

  • Vehicle Red Zones: Private vehicles, taxis, and buses are not allowed along Ram Path, Bhakti Path, and near Naya Ghat. Taxis from Lucknow or Varanasi must drop passengers at designated parking lots outside the city (e.g. Saket Petrol Pump area).
  • Local Commute: Only authorized local E-Rickshaws are permitted inside the barricaded zones. E-Rickshaw fares range from ₹50 to ₹100 per head during festival days.
  • Walking Paths: Devotees should be prepared to walk 1 to 2 km to reach the Ram Mandir gates from the vehicle drop points.

Tips for a Smooth Diwali Yatra with Family

Crucial Advice for Families:
  1. Book Hotels 3-4 Months Early: Hotels and dharamshalas in Ayodhya are 100% booked weeks before Diwali. Check our Ayodhya Yatra Cost and Budget Planner to calculate stay estimates.
  2. Stay Close to Ram Path: Book accommodations within walking distance of the main corridor to avoid rickshaw availability issues.
  3. Senior Citizen Care: Avoid taking very young children or elderly family members to the crowded Saryu Ghat steps on the main Deepotsav night. Instead, view the lighting from a safe distance or choose a hotel terrace view.

Frequently Asked Questions

Ayodhya Deepotsav kab hota hai?

Deepotsav Diwali ke ek din pehle (Choti Diwali/Narak Chaturdashi) ko Ram Ki Paidi aur Saryu Ghat par aayojit kiya jata hai, jahan lakho diyas jalaye jaate hain. Diwali ke din main deep daan aarti hoti hai.

Kya Diwali ke din Ram Mandir khula rehta hai?

Haan, Diwali ke din Ram Mandir poore samay khula rehta hai, lekin vishesh aarti aur bhog ke samay aam shradhaluo ke liye pravesh kuch samay ke liye band kiya jata hai.

Diwali ke dauran Ayodhya me transport kaise milta hai?

Diwali ke dauran Lucknow Airport se direct private AC cabs (Innova/Dzire) book karna best hai, jo aapko outer checkpost tak drop karengi. Local auto aur e-rickshaws wahan se aage jaane ka best sadhan hain. Check out the Lucknow to Ayodhya taxi fare guide for booking options.
