2. Recommended Day-by-Day Itineraries

Depending on your flight or train schedule, we offer two perfectly timed itineraries designed to avoid highway rush and ensure peaceful temple darshans:

🌟 Option A: 4-Day / 3-Night Relaxed Family Itinerary (Top Seller)

Best for: Families traveling with parents, senior citizens, and pilgrims seeking in-depth darshan of both Ram Janmabhoomi and Kashi Vishwanath.

Day 1: Arrival in Ayodhya, Ram Janmabhoomi & Saryu Aarti

  • Pickup from Ayodhya Dham Airport (AYX) or Railway Station. Private AC transfer to your 3-star hotel.
  • Afternoon visit to Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra for divine darshan of Ram Lalla. Climb 76 steps to Hanuman Garhi to seek Bajrangbali's blessings.
  • Evening witness the grand Saryu Maha Aarti at Naya Ghat / Ram Ki Paidi. Overnight stay in Ayodhya.

Day 2: Kanak Bhawan & Scenic Highway Drive to Varanasi (200 km)

  • Morning visit to Kanak Bhawan (golden palace of Sita Ram) and the serene Guptar Ghat.
  • 11:00 AM: Depart for Varanasi via NH-330 (approx. 3.5 to 4 hours smooth 4-lane highway drive). Lunch halt near Sultanpur.
  • 03:30 PM: Check-in at your hotel in Varanasi. Fresh up and relax.
  • 06:00 PM: Board a private boat at Dashashwamedh Ghat to experience the spellbinding Ganga Maha Aarti from the riverfront. Overnight stay in Varanasi.

Day 3: Kashi Vishwanath Sugam Darshan & Sacred Temples of Kashi

  • 07:00 AM: VIP Sugam Darshan of Shri Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga via Corridor Gate No. 4. Visit Maa Annapurna and Vishalakshi Devi Shaktipeeth.
  • 11:00 AM: Darshan at Kaal Bhairav Mandir (Kotwal of Kashi; mandatory pilgrimage ritual).
  • Afternoon: Visit Sankat Mochan Hanuman Mandir, Durga Kund Mandir, and the magnificent marble New Vishwanath Temple (BHU). Overnight stay in Varanasi.

Day 4: Subah-e-Banaras, Sarnath Excursion & Departure

  • 05:30 AM: Sunrise boat tour from Assi Ghat to Manikarnika Ghat with classical music and Vedic chanting at Subah-e-Banaras.
  • 09:30 AM: Excursion to Sarnath (10 km). Visit Dhamek Stupa, Deer Park, and the Archaeological Museum (Lion Capital of Ashoka).
  • 02:30 PM onwards: Transfer to Varanasi Airport (VNS) or Railway Station for your onward journey with divine memories.

3. The Highway Journey: Ayodhya to Varanasi (Route & Travel Time)

The 200 km road journey between Ayodhya and Varanasi has been transformed into one of Uttar Pradesh's smoothest highway corridors:

🛣️ Route 1: Via NH-330 & NH-31 (Recommended)

Distance: 198 km | Time: 3.5 to 4 Hours
Passes via Sultanpur and Jaunpur. Completely 4-laned, bypasses congested city centers, with excellent roadside food plazas and clean restrooms.

🛣️ Route 2: Via Purvanchal Expressway Link

Distance: 215 km | Time: 3.5 Hours
Accesses the access-controlled Purvanchal Expressway near Bikapur, connecting towards Varanasi via Ghazipur / Jaunpur link. Zero traffic delays.

🚆 Train vs. Cab Comparison

While Vande Bharat Express connects Ayodhya and Varanasi, tickets sell out weeks in advance. A private cab provides total flexibility for luggage, door-to-door hotel drops, and intermediate sightseeing.

6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the best time to visit Ayodhya and Varanasi together?

The ideal season is from October to March when temperatures range between 12°C and 25°C, making afternoon temple walking and morning sunrise boat rides extremely pleasant. Major festivals during this time include Ayodhya Deepotsav, Kartik Parikrama, Dev Deepawali in Varanasi, and Maha Shivratri.

Can we also cover Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam) between Ayodhya and Varanasi?

Yes! By extending the itinerary from 4 Days to 5 Days, you can easily cover the complete Holy Trinity (Ayodhya -> Prayagraj -> Varanasi) with a holy snan at Triveni Sangam and darshan of Lete Hue Hanuman Ji.

Are there luggage restrictions in the private tour cabs?

For sedans (Dzire), 2 large trolley bags plus 2 hand bags fit easily. For Ertiga or Innova Crysta, 4 to 5 large bags can be accommodated with foldable rear seating or roof carriers upon request.
