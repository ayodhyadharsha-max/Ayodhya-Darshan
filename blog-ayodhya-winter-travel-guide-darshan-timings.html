Ayodhya Ram Mandir Winter Travel Guide (2026–2027)

Published by Ayodhya Dharshan Editorial Team | Updated October 15, 2026

Peak Devotion Season: Winter is widely regarded as the most comfortable and spiritually vibrant time to explore Ayodhya Dham. With clear afternoon skies and temperatures hovering around 20°C, walking between sacred sites like Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan, and Ram Janmabhoomi is pleasant and energizing.

1. Winter Daily Aarti & Darshan Schedule

The Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra Trust follows a specialized seasonal schedule during winter months to accommodate cooler sunrise temperatures and ritual bhog offerings:

Aarti / Darshan Event Winter Timing Entry Pass Requirement
Mangala Aarti 04:30 AM – 05:00 AM Mandatory Free Pass (Report by 04:00 AM)
Shringar Aarti 06:30 AM – 07:00 AM Aarti Pass Required
Morning General Darshan 07:00 AM – 11:30 AM Open Queue or Sugam Darshan Slot
Bhog Aarti (Afternoon) 12:00 PM – 12:30 PM Pass Holders & Devotees Present
Evening Darshan & Sandhya Aarti 07:30 PM – 08:00 PM Aarti Pass / Open Darshan till 09:30 PM
Shayan Aarti 10:00 PM Final Day Aarti (Pass Required)

2. Packing Essentials for Winter Devotees

Because leather items (belts, wallets, leather shoes, jackets) are strictly prohibited inside the security checkpoints of the temple complex, smart winter packing is essential:

  • Thermal Innerwear: High-grade thermal tops and bottoms are the best way to stay warm under traditional kurtas, sarees, or dhotis.
  • Non-Leather Slip-On Footwear: Footwear must be deposited at the free locker counters. Slip-on shoes or crocs save valuable time in cold morning queues.
  • Woollen Socks with Rubber Grip: Cold stone flooring in open courtyards can be tough on bare feet. Wearing fresh, clean woollen socks is permitted and highly recommended.
  • Mufflers & Shawls: Light woollen shawls are permitted through scanners and keep senior citizens comfortable during evening Saryu Aarti at Ram Ki Paidi.

3. Fog Travel Advisory for Flights & Trains

Between mid-December and late January, North Indian winter fog can cause visibility drops during morning hours:

  • Vande Bharat & Express Trains: Trains departing Delhi, Lucknow, or Patna early in the morning may run with 30–60 minute delays. Keep a flexible buffer in your first-day itinerary.
  • Maharishi Valmiki International Airport (AYJ): Afternoon flights typically operate on schedule, whereas early morning departures may experience fog-related rescheduling.
  • Private AC Highway Cabs: Our professional highway chauffeurs are experienced with fog navigation on NH27, utilizing fog lamps and GPS to guarantee safe airport and hotel transfers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What are the winter darshan timings at Ayodhya Ram Mandir?

General darshan is open from 7:00 AM to 11:30 AM and resumes from 2:00 PM to 9:30 PM. Mangala Aarti passes require entry by 4:00 AM.

Q2: Can senior citizens get wheelchair assistance during winter?

Yes, free wheelchair assistance and battery-operated carts (golf carts) are available at the entrance gate for senior citizens and differently-abled pilgrims.

Q3: What time is the evening Saryu Aarti held in winter?

The Grand Saryu Maha Aarti at Ram Ki Paidi and Naya Ghat commences at 6:00 PM during winter. Pilgrims are advised to arrive by 5:30 PM for prime seating on the ghat steps.

Q4: Which is the best month for an Ayodhya pilgrimage?

November, December, January, and February are the most pleasant months, featuring comfortable daytime weather ideal for temple parikrama and outdoor sightseeing.
