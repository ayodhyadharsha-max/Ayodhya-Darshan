Ayodhya Airport to Ram Mandir Distance & Taxi Fare Guide (2026)

Published by Ayodhya Dharshan Editorial Team | Updated October 2026

3. Recommended Route by Road

The standard driving route from Maharishi Valmiki Airport goes via NH28 (Gorakhpur-Lucknow Highway) onto Ram Path. Traffic moves smoothly along the newly widened 4-lane pilgrimage corridor, taking you straight to the main temple drop-off point near Sugam Darshan Pass counters.
