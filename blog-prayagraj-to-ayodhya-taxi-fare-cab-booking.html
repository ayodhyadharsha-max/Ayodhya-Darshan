2. Highway Route & Driving Conditions (NH330 via Sultanpur)

The journey from Prayagraj to Ayodhya follows National Highway 330 (NH330):

  • Route: Prayagraj → Phaphamau → Pratapgarh Bypass → Sultanpur → Bhadaiya → Ayodhya Dham.
  • Driving Duration: Approximately 3 hours 20 minutes to 3 hours 45 minutes on smooth, paved 4-lane bypasses.
  • Clean Food Stops: Several reputable pure vegetarian family restaurants and dhabas are situated along the Sultanpur highway corridor.
  • Direct Drop: Avoid local auto bargaining in Ayodhya—our chauffeurs drop you right at your booked hotel lobby or near the Janmabhoomi entry point.

3. Combine Prayagraj Snan with Kashi & Ayodhya Circuit

Devotees across India seek the holy trinity of UP teerth kshetras:
