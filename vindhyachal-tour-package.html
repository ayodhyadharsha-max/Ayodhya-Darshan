Frequently Asked Questions (Vindhyachal)

How far is Vindhyachal from Varanasi?

Vindhyachal is just 65 km (1.5 hours drive) from Varanasi, making it an ideal day excursion.

What is Trikon Parikrama?

Trikon Parikrama is the holy triangular circuit connecting three manifestation temples: Maa Vindhyavasini, Maa Kali (Kali Khoh), and Maa Saraswati (Ashtabhuja).

Is ropeway facility available at Ashtabhuja Devi?

Yes, modern electric ropeway car facility is available for effortless hilltop access.
