Varanasi Dev Deepawali 2026 Boat Booking & VIP Ghat Pass Rates

Viral Festival Guide6 min readPublished September 2026

Celebrated on Kartik Purnima (fifteen days after Diwali), Dev Deepawali in Varanasi (Kashi) is often hailed as the "Diwali of the Gods." On this divine night, all 84 historic ghats of Varanasi light up with over 10 Lakh glittering earthen oil lamps (diyas), accompanied by grand Ganga Maha Aarti, green fireworks, and laser light shows.

The single best way to experience Dev Deepawali is from a private boat or Ganga river cruise. In this complete guide, we cover Dev Deepawali 2026 boat booking options, rates, VIP ghat access, and Ayodhya-Kashi tour packages.

Combine Varanasi Dev Deepawali with Ayodhya Ram Mandir Yatra

Most devotees plan a 4 Days / 3 Nights Combined Circuit covering both Dev Deepawali in Kashi and Ram Lalla Darshan in Ayodhya:

  • Day 1: Arrive in Varanasi, check in to hotel, evening Dev Deepawali boat ride & Aarti.
  • Day 2: Early morning Kashi Vishwanath VIP Darshan, Kaal Bhairav & Sarnath tour.
  • Day 3: Morning drive to Ayodhya (4 hours), visit Hanuman Garhi & Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir.
  • Day 4: Saryu Snan, Kanak Bhawan & departure transfer.
