Ayodhya Deepotsav 2026 Traffic Advisory: Road Diversions & Parking Guide

Published by Ayodhya Dharshan Editorial Team | Updated October 29, 2026

⚡ Quick Snapshot: Traffic Rules for Ayodhya Deepotsav (Nov 5 – 8, 2026)

  • Peak Restriction Date: Saturday, November 7, 2026 (Main Deepotsav Evening)
  • Zero-Vehicle Pedestrian Zones: Beyond Saket Petrol Pump Barrier, Tedhi Bazar, and Dharampath
  • NH-27 Highway Diversions: Heavy commercial vehicles diverted via Purvanchal Expressway; light vehicles guided to outer rings
  • Primary Satellite Parking: Tedhi Bazar Multi-Level, Saket College Ground, Chauka Ghat & Naka Bypass
  • Average Walking Distance: 1.5 to 2.5 km from satellite parking lots to Ram Mandir & Saryu Ghats (Shuttle e-rickshaws available)
  • Ayodhya Deepotsav Cab Helpline: Direct assistance via WhatsApp: +91 92352 22399

With over 25 lakh pilgrims, dignitaries, and tourists arriving in Ayodhya for the grand 10th milestone edition of Ayodhya Deepotsav 2026 on November 7, the Ayodhya Police Commissionerate and District Administration have implemented a comprehensive, multi-tiered traffic diversion plan.

Whether you are driving your personal car or traveling by private cab from Lucknow, Gorakhpur, Varanasi, or Prayagraj, understanding where vehicles are allowed, which roads are pedestrian-only, and where to safely park your car is vital to avoid hours of traffic congestion. In this detailed guide, we provide the official road breakdown, designated satellite parking lots, shuttle arrangements, and verified Ayodhya tour package cab options.

1. Zero-Vehicle Zones & Road Closures (Nov 7, 2026)

To ensure smooth movement of the grand Shobha Yatra (procession of 18 Ramayana tableaux) and maintain security around the Guinness World Record setup at Ram Ki Paidi, several key corridors are declared strictly pedestrian-only:

🚫 Saket Petrol Pump Checkpoint

Vehicular traffic moving from Faizabad city/Cantt towards Naya Ghat is completely halted at the Saket barrier. No private cars, taxis, or standard e-rickshaws are permitted beyond this point towards Ram Katha Park or Ram Ki Paidi.

🚫 Tedhi Bazar (Guru Vashishth Chowk)

Acts as the western barricade. Vehicles heading from the bypass towards Janmabhoomi Path or Hanuman Garhi must turn into the designated multi-level parking facility.

🚫 Dharma Path & Ram Path Corridors

Converted into security corridors from 8:00 AM on November 7 until midnight. Only vehicles bearing valid administration/VIP security passes and emergency ambulances are permitted.

🚫 Naya Ghat & Veer Savarkar Setu

The bridge over River Saryu connecting Gonda to Ayodhya is closed to private motorized traffic on the evening of November 7 to prioritize pedestrian crowds viewing the Mega Drone Show.

2. Highway Diversions on NH-27 (Lucknow – Gorakhpur Route)

National Highway 27 (NH-27) is the primary lifeline connecting Lucknow and Gorakhpur through Ayodhya. Heavy restrictions will be active from November 5 to November 8, 2026:

  • Heavy Freight & Commercial Trucks: Completely prohibited from entering Ayodhya. Trucks from Lucknow are diverted at Barabanki via Jarwal-Bahraich or Purvanchal Expressway. Trucks from Gorakhpur are diverted at Basti and Kalwari towards Tanda/Ambedkar Nagar.
  • Passenger Buses & Light Motor Vehicles (LMVs): Allowed up to peripheral satellite interchanges. Private cars with bookings in inner Ayodhya hotels must arrive before 10:00 AM on November 7 to secure hotel parking before barricades seal the central roads.

4. Essential Tips to Avoid Getting Stranded on Deepotsav Night

  1. Mobile Network Congestion Warning:
    With over 20 lakh people packed between Ram Ki Paidi and Naya Ghat, cellular data and phone call signals experience severe throttling between 6:30 PM and 10:00 PM. Pre-fix an exact physical meeting point (e.g., a specific shop near Saket Petrol Pump or Tedhi Bazar parking) with your driver and family members.
  2. Arrive Before 11:00 AM:
    The grand Shobha Yatra begins from Saket College at 12:00 PM. Traffic movement slows down drastically after 11:00 AM. Arriving early ensures you park smoothly and complete your afternoon temple visits before evening restrictions tighten.
  3. Wear Comfortable Walking Footwear:
    Expect to walk 3 to 5 kilometers in total between satellite parking lots, temple complexes, and the Saryu viewing ghats.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Can outstation cabs drop passengers directly at Ram Mandir on Deepotsav?

No. All non-permit vehicles are stopped at outer perimeter checkpoints like Saket Petrol Pump and Tedhi Bazar. Pilgrims must cover the remaining distance on foot or via electric shuttle golf carts.

Q2: What is the parking fee at the official satellite parking lots?

Official municipal parking lots like Tedhi Bazar Multi-Level Parking charge nominal government-regulated rates (typically ₹50 for 4-wheelers and ₹20 for 2-wheelers), while open mela grounds managed by district authorities are often free.

Q3: When will regular traffic resume after the Deepotsav celebrations?

Road restrictions on Dharma Path and Ram Path ease past midnight on November 7 as crowds disperse, though inner heritage lanes remain pedestrianized for Diwali festivities on November 8.

Q4: Are emergency ambulances allowed through barricades?

Yes, dedicated green corridors are maintained along Ram Path and NH-27 for ambulances, fire tenders, and police emergency response vehicles at all times.
