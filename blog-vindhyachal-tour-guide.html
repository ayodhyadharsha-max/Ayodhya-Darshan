Vindhyachal Yatra Guide 2026: Maa Vindhyavasini, Trikon Parikrama & Cab Fares

Shakti Peeth Guide5 min readPublished September 2026

Located on the bank of River Ganga in Mirzapur district of Uttar Pradesh, Vindhyachal Dham is one of the most powerful Shakti Peeths in India. It is believed that Goddess Mahalakshmi resides here in the form of Maa Vindhyavasini Devi.

Devotees from around the world visit Vindhyachal to complete the sacred Trikon Yatra (Triangle Pilgrimage), which connects the three cosmic energies of Laxmi, Kali, and Saraswati.

🌺 The Sacred Trikon Parikrama Shrines

The Trikon Parikrama is a holy triangular pilgrimage covering approximately 12 kilometers across the Vindhya hill ranges, representing the three prime forms of the Supreme Divine Mother:

  • 1. Maa Vindhyavasini Temple (Goddess Mahalakshmi): Situated along the holy river Ganga in Vindhyachal town. Unlike other Shakti Peeths where body parts of Mata Sati fell, it is believed that Goddess Durga chose to establish her permanent abode here after slaying Mahishasura. The sanctum features an awe-inspiring black stone idol adorned with silver crown and golden eyes.
  • 2. Kali Khoh Temple (Goddess Mahakali): Located 2 km from Vindhyavasini in a tranquil valley cave surrounded by verdant forests. Goddess Kali is worshipped here in her fierce, protective roop as the slayer of demons Chanda and Munda.
  • 3. Ashtabhuja Devi Temple (Goddess Mahasaraswati): Positioned atop a hillock 3 km from Kali Khoh. The shrine is dedicated to the eight-armed infant goddess who slipped from Kansa's hands in Mathura and manifested in the Vindhya mountains. Devotees climb 150 stone steps or use the modern ropeway.

⏰ Official Temple & Aarti Timings

Maa Vindhyavasini Temple remains open throughout the day except during specific shringar and bhog intervals:

Aarti / Darshan Period Timings Ritual Significance
Mangala Aarti 04:00 AM – 05:00 AM First morning awakening and snan ritual.
Morning Darshan 05:00 AM – 12:00 PM Open for public darshan, offerings, and abhishek.
Bhog & Shringar Aarti 12:00 PM – 01:30 PM Midday sacred food offering; doors temporarily closed.
Afternoon Darshan 01:30 PM – 07:15 PM General public darshan resumed.
Sandhya (Evening) Aarti 07:15 PM – 08:15 PM Grand evening deepa arti with conch and bells.
Shayan Aarti & Closing 09:30 PM – 10:30 PM Night rest ceremony; temple closes at 10:30 PM.

🚡 Vindhyachal Aerial Ropeway (Kali Khoh to Ashtabhuja)

For the convenience of elderly devotees, pregnant women, and pilgrims, Uttar Pradesh Tourism operates a scenic aerial ropeway (cable car) connecting Kali Khoh Temple to Ashtabhuja Temple hill. The ropeway glides above green forest valleys in about 6 minutes, offering panoramic views of the Vindhya plateau and the meandering Ganga.

  • Ropeway Operating Hours: 07:00 AM to 06:30 PM daily.
  • Fare: Approx ₹60 – ₹70 per person for round-trip. Tickets are available at the base counter.

🚗 How to Reach Vindhyachal Dham

  • From Varanasi: 65 km (1.5 to 2 hours drive via NH-19). Perfect for a same-day return pilgrimage from Varanasi.
  • From Prayagraj: 85 km (2 to 2.5 hours drive via Mirzapur Highway). Many pilgrims cover Prayagraj Sangam bath in the morning and reach Vindhyachal by afternoon.
  • From Ayodhya: 220 km (approx 4.5 hours drive via Sultanpur-Pratapgarh-Mirzapur route). Easily integrated into the Full Ramayana Circuit.
  • Nearest Railway Station: Vindhyachal (BDL) station is 1 km away, and Mirzapur (MZP) junction is 8 km away, with direct trains from Delhi, Kolkata, and Mumbai.
  • Nearest Airport: Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport, Varanasi (VNS), located 75 km away.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How long does the complete Trikon Parikrama take?

By private AC cab, the full Trikon Parikrama (covering all three shrines and darshan) takes around 3 to 4 hours. Devotees walking barefoot typically take 5 to 6 hours.

2. Can Vindhyachal be combined with a Varanasi tour?

Yes. A same-day private cab from Varanasi is the most popular way. Depart Varanasi around 7:00 AM, reach Vindhyachal by 8:30 AM, complete Trikon Darshan, have sattvic lunch, and return to Varanasi by 4:00 PM in time for Dashashwamedh Ghat evening Ganga Aarti.

3. Are locker and footwear facilities available near the temple?

Yes. The new Vindhya Corridor provides secure footwear stands, luggage cloakrooms, drinking water points, and clean restrooms near the entry plazas.
