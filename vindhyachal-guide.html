Vindhyachal Shaktipeeth — Complete Spiritual & Trikon Guide

Complete spiritual & travel guide to Vindhyachal Shaktipeeth. Learn about Vindhyavasini Devi Mandir, Ashtabhuja, Kali Khoh & Holy Trikon Parikrama.

Vindhyachal Dham, located along the Ganga River in Mirzapur district, is one of the most powerful Shaktipeeths in India where Goddess Durga resides in her eternal form as Ma Vindhyavasini.

Whether you are seeking spiritual peace, ancient temple darshan, or sacred river dip, this guide provides complete information on divine sights and heritage.

1. Ma Vindhyavasini Devi Mandir

Unlike other Shaktipeeths where body parts of Sati fell, Vindhyachal is the exact place where Goddess Durga manifested after defeating Mahishasura. The black stone idol adorned with silver crowns is worshipped 24x7.

2. Sacred Trikon Parikrama

The holy triangular pilgrimage (Trikon Yatra) covers three divine shrines nestled amidst the Vindhya hills: Ma Vindhyavasini (Maha Lakshmi), Ma Saraswati (Ashtabhuja Devi), and Ma Kali (Kali Khoh Temple).

3. Ashtabhuja Devi & Sita Kund

Perched atop a scenic hill lock, Ashtabhuja Temple honors the 8-armed Goddess who escaped from Kansa hands. Nearby Sita Kund is a natural fresh spring associated with Mata Sita.

4. Kali Khoh & Ganga Ghats

Nestled inside a natural mountain cave, Kali Khoh Temple honors Goddess Kali. Below, the sacred Ganga Ghats of Mirzapur offer peaceful boat rides and morning holy dips.
