Ayodhya Electric Bus Routes, Ticket Fare & Timings (2026)

Published by Ayodhya Dharshan Editorial Team | Updated October 2026

Official Local Transport: 100+ eco-friendly zero-emission AC Electric Buses run across Ayodhya Dham connecting Railway Stations, Airport, Ram Mandir, and Saryu Aarti Ghats from 5:30 AM to 10:30 PM.

1. Major Ayodhya E-Bus Routes & Halts

The Uttar Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation (UPSRTC) operates low-floor AC electric buses connecting all key points across Ayodhya city. Key routes include:

Route Name Key Bus Halts & Stops Frequency Ticket Fare
Route 1: Airport Special Ayodhya Airport – Ram Path – Hanuman Garhi – Naya Ghat Every 15 mins ₹35
Route 2: Station Shuttle Ayodhya Cantt (FD) – Tedhi Bazaar – Ram Mandir Gate – Ayodhya Dham (AY) Every 10 mins ₹15 – ₹25
Route 3: Parikrama Circuit Naya Ghat – Ram Ki Paidi – Mani Parvat – Guptar Ghat – Bus Stand Every 12 mins ₹20

2. Ticket Price Structure

  • Up to 3 km: ₹10
  • 3 km to 6 km: ₹15
  • 6 km to 10 km: ₹25
  • Airport / Full City Length (10+ km): ₹35 – ₹50

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How do I pay for electric bus tickets in Ayodhya?

You can pay cash directly to the bus conductor or use UPI payment (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm) on POS ticketing machines.

Q2: Are electric buses in Ayodhya air-conditioned?

Yes, 100% of Ayodhya city electric buses are fully air-conditioned (AC) with comfortable seating and wheelchair accessibility.
