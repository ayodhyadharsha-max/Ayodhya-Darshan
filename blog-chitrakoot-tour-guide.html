Chitrakoot Yatra Guide 2026: Kamadgiri, Ramghat & Gupt Godavari Tour

Ramayana Yatra Guide6 min readPublished September 2026

Nestled along the quiet banks of the Mandakini River on the Vindhya mountain range, Chitrakoot Dham is one of the most revered pilgrimage destinations in Ramayana lore. It is here that Lord Shri Ram, Mata Sita, and Lakshman ji spent 11 and a half years of their 14-year exile (Ram Vanvas).

Whether you are planning a 1-day excursion from Prayagraj or a comprehensive 2-day Ramayana circuit tour, this complete Chitrakoot Yatra Guide 2026 covers top sightseeing places, Kamadgiri Parikrama tips, Ramghat boat rides, Gupt Godavari caves, and package details.

📍 Top Sightseeing Places to Visit in Chitrakoot

  • Ramghat & Mandakini Aarti: The central ghat where Saint Tulsidas ji had the divine darshan of Lord Ram. Enjoy a peaceful wooden motorboat ride and witness evening floating lamp Aarti.
  • Kamadgiri Parikrama & Kamtanath Mandir: Perform the 5 km holy parikrama around the wish-fulfilling Kamadgiri hill. Shaded and paved paths make it easy for all age groups.
  • Gupt Godavari Caves: Natural limestone pair caves where an underground stream of Mandakini flows through narrow rock tunnels.
  • Hanuman Dhara Waterfall & Ropeway: A scenic hilltop shrine accessed via modern ropeway, where a natural mountain spring falls continuously over Lord Hanuman.
  • Sphatik Shila & Janki Kund: Sacred rock along Mandakini river bearing the footprints of Lord Ram and Mata Sita.
  • Sati Anusuya Ashram: Quiet forest ashram of Maharshi Atri and Mata Anusuya, surrounded by dense greenery.
