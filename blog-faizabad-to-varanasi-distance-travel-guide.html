Faizabad to Varanasi Distance: Complete Travel & Route Guide (2026)

Published by Ayodhya Dharshan Editorial Team | Updated October 2026

Quick Summary: The distance from Faizabad (Ayodhya Cantt) to Varanasi (Banaras) is 195 km by road and 202 km by train. Travel time takes around 3.5 to 4.5 hours by cab or express train via NH330/NH731.

1. Faizabad to Varanasi Distance & Travel Overview

Whether you are starting your pilgrimage from Faizabad Junction (Ayodhya Cantt - AYC) or staying near Faizabad city center, traveling to Varanasi (Kashi Vishwanath Temple) is one of the most popular travel routes in Uttar Pradesh. Below is a comparative table of transport options:

2. Faizabad (Ayodhya Cantt) to Varanasi Train Schedule

Direct trains run daily between Ayodhya Cantt (AYC / Faizabad) and Varanasi Junction (BSB) / Banaras (BSBS). Top train connections include:

  • Farakka Express (13484): Departs AYC daily around 19:40, arrives Varanasi Junction at 23:45 (Time: 4h 05m).
  • Sabarmati Express (19167): Runs 4 days a week, connects Faizabad to Varanasi in approx 4h 15m.
  • Marudhar Express (14864): Departs AYC at 02:40 AM, reaches Banaras (BSBS) at 07:00 AM.
  • Vande Bharat / Express options: Frequent intercity expresses connect Ayodhya Dham Junction & Ayodhya Cantt directly to Varanasi in under 3.5 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: How far is Faizabad from Varanasi Kashi Vishwanath?

The distance is 195 km by road via NH330 and NH731. It takes about 3 hours 45 minutes to reach Kashi Vishwanath Temple gates from Faizabad.

Q2: Is Faizabad station the same as Ayodhya Cantt?

Yes, Faizabad Junction was officially renamed to Ayodhya Cantt (Station Code: AYC). It serves both Faizabad city and Ayodhya Dham.
