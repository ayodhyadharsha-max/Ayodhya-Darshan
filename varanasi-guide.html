Varanasi (Kashi) — Complete Spiritual & Sacred Sightseeing Guide

Complete spiritual & travel guide to Varanasi (Kashi). Learn about Kashi Vishwanath Mandir, Dashashwamedh Ganga Aarti, Kaal Bhairav, Sarnath & Ghats.

Varanasi (Kashi), one of the world oldest continually inhabited cities, is the spiritual heart of Sanatan Dharma situated on the crescent-shaped banks of the holy River Ganga.

Whether you are seeking spiritual peace, ancient temple darshan, or sacred river dip, this guide provides complete information on divine sights and heritage.

1. Shri Kashi Vishwanath Mandir (Jyotirlinga)

The divine abode of Lord Shiva, Shri Kashi Vishwanath Mandir is one of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas. Enter through Gate No. 4 (Chhatta Dwar) for priority Sugam entry. Sparsh Darshan is available during early morning hours.

2. Dashashwamedh Ghat & Ganga Aarti

Every evening at 6:30 PM, Dashashwamedh Ghat transforms into a grand arena of divine light during the famous Ganga Aarti. Magnificent brass lamps, incense, and Vedic chants illuminate the riverbank.

3. Kaal Bhairav & Annapurna Mandir

Known as the Kotwal (Guardian) of Kashi, visiting Kaal Bhairav Mandir is considered essential for completing a pilgrimage to Varanasi. Nearby Shri Annapurna Mandir is dedicated to the Goddess of Nourishment.

4. Sarnath & Dhamek Stupa

Located just 10 km from Varanasi main city, Sarnath is where Bhagwan Buddha preached his first sermon after attaining enlightenment. Key sights include Dhamek Stupa and Ashoka Pillar.
