Varanasi Air Show 2026: Wings Over Ganges Dates, Namo Ghat Timings, Free Entry & Viewing Guide

Published by Ayodhya Dharshan Editorial Team | Updated October 20, 2026

⚡ Quick Overview: Wings Over Ganges (Varanasi Air Show 2026)

  • Event Name: Indian Air Force Mega Aerial Display — "Wings Over Ganges"
  • Primary Venue: Namo Ghat (नमो घाट) & the Ganga Riverfront, Varanasi, UP
  • Key Dates: October 22 & 24, 2026 (Rehearsals) | October 25, 2026 (Grand Public Air Show)
  • Display Timings: 10:30 AM to 1:30 PM (Rehearsals) | 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM (Main Grand Show)
  • Entry Fee: 100% FREE for Public (No tickets or registration required)
  • Star Performers: Surya Kiran Aerobatic Team (9 Hawks), Sarang Helicopter Display Team (Dhruv), Rafale, Su-30MKI, LCA Tejas & LCH Prachand
  • Ayodhya to Varanasi Cab Assistance: Direct AC taxis available 24x7 via WhatsApp: +91 92352 22399

The spiritual capital of India is preparing to witness history in the skies. In a grand celebration of India's aerospace strength against the majestic backdrop of the world's oldest living city, the Indian Air Force (IAF) is staging a breathtaking mega air display titled "Wings Over Ganges" at Namo Ghat, Varanasi.

For four days between October 22 and October 25, 2026, supersonic fighter jets, precision aerobatic formations, and heavy tactical transports will carve tricolour smoke trails above the sacred waters of Mother Ganga and historic temples. Whether you are a local resident of Kashi, an aviation enthusiast, or a pilgrim traveling between Ayodhya Ram Mandir and Kashi Vishwanath Dham, here is your definitive spectator and travel guide.

1. Schedule of Events: Dates & Daily Timings

The IAF Central Air Command in coordination with the Varanasi District Administration and Police Commissionerate has outlined a structured schedule to allow lakhs of spectators to enjoy the spectacles safely:

Date Event Phase Public Timings What to Expect
Thursday, Oct 22, 2026 First Full Practice & Sorties 10:30 AM – 1:30 PM Fighter flypasts, route reconnaissance, and preliminary diamond formation practice.
Saturday, Oct 24, 2026 Full Dress Rehearsal 10:30 AM – 1:30 PM Complete synchronized routine with full aerobatics, smoke trails, and mock paratrooper drops.
Sunday, Oct 25, 2026 Grand Public Air Show (Wings Over Ganges) 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM The premier aerial showcase with Rafale, Su-30MKI sonic climbs, Surya Kiran team, and Sarang display.

*Tip: Exact flying windows may adjust by 15–30 minutes subject to weather clearance, wind speeds along the river basin, and air traffic control coordination.

2. Participating Aircraft & Aerobatic Display Lineup

The display will bring India's elite frontline air defense and indigenous engineering to the Varanasi river basin:

✈️ Surya Kiran Aerobatic Team (SKAT)

Flying 9 British Aerospace Hawk Mk132 advanced jet trainers in close-proximity diamond and arrowhead formations. Watch out for their signature barrel-rolls, loop maneuvers, and breathtaking criss-cross flypasts leaving saffron, white, and green plumes over the Ganga.

🚁 Sarang Helicopter Display Team

Piloting 4 indigenous HAL Dhruv Advanced Light Helicopters (ALH). Painted in bright peacock-inspired livery, the Sarang team performs delicate airborne pirouettes, reverse taxi maneuvers, and the thrilling cross-under pass.

⚡ Supersonic Fighters: Rafale, Su-30MKI & Tejas

The mighty Dassault Rafale and Sukhoi Su-30MKI will demonstrate super-maneuverability with tail-slides, vertical climbs into the clouds, and low-altitude high-speed passes. India's indigenous pride, the LCA Tejas fighter, will also display agile tactical turns.

🪂 Akash Ganga Skydiving & Heavy Transports

The elite Akash Ganga paratroopers will leap from an AN-32 transport aircraft at 8,000 feet, performing canopied formations before precision landings along designated riverbank sand bars. Look out also for the giant C-295 and IL-76 Gajraj flypasts.

3. Ticket Price, Passes & Fraud Warning

⚠️ Urgent Advisory: Entry is 100% FREE — Beware of Scams

The district administration of Varanasi and the IAF have strictly clarified that no tickets, passes, or registration fees are required to watch the air show. Any website, WhatsApp group, or middleman claiming to sell "VIP Pass for Varanasi Air Show" or "Reserved Front Row Seats at Namo Ghat" is committing fraud. All designated public viewing zones along the ghats and bridges are open to all visitors on a first-come, first-served basis.

4. Top 6 Viewing Spots in Varanasi for the Best View & Photos

The natural amphitheatre of Varanasi’s crescent-shaped ghats offers thousands of vantage points. Here are the top 6 locations for photographers and families:

  1. Namo Ghat (The Official Event Hub):
    With its expansive paved promenades, iconic giant 'Namaste' sculpture, floating CNG station, and amphitheatre steps, Namo Ghat is ground zero where the official commentary, VIP stage, and prime visual axis are positioned. Reach at least 90 minutes before showtime to get front-row river-facing spots.
  2. Rajghat & Malviya Bridge:
    Situated immediately adjacent to Namo Ghat, the steps of Rajghat and the pedestrian walkways of the historic double-decker Malviya (Dufferin) Bridge offer elevated, unobstructed panoramic views of aircraft diving down towards the river.
  3. River Ganga Tourist Boats & Electric Catamarans:
    Watching the air show from a registered wooden Bajra, motorboat, or luxury electric catamaran anchored in the middle of the holy river is an unforgettable experience. Note that river movement will be regulated by the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) and Jal Police during active flying slots, so board early from Dashashwamedh or Assi Ghat.
  4. Eastern Sand Banks (Across the Ganga River):
    For professional photographers carrying heavy telephoto lenses (300mm to 600mm), taking a boat across to the open sandy riverbed on the eastern bank provides zero crowd obstruction, low horizon lines, and ideal natural lighting for flight passes.
  5. Dashashwamedh Ghat & Man Mandir Observatory Terrace:
    While further south, the broad stairs of Dashashwamedh and the elevated terrace of Raja Man Singh’s 17th-century observatory offer splendid sightlines for high-altitude loopings and turnarounds.
  6. Assi Ghat & Ravidas Smarak:
    Ideal for travelers staying in South Varanasi or Lanka (BHU). Fighters making long circuit passes frequently sweep low over the Assi to Ramnagar stretch before turning north into their presentation run.

5. Traffic Advisory, Parking & Security Regulations

  • Strict No-Fly Zone for Private Drones: In accordance with DGCA and Ministry of Civil Aviation rules, the entire airspace over Varanasi is declared a high-security Red Zone. Flying personal camera drones anywhere near the ghats or city is strictly banned and will result in equipment confiscation and FIR registration.
  • Vehicle Diversions: Direct vehicular traffic towards Rajghat and Namo Ghat will be restricted 2 hours prior to the flight window. Private vehicles will be diverted towards designated multi-level parking grounds at Sampurnanand University, Chauka Ghat, and Cantt Station perimeter.
  • What to Carry: Wide-brim sun hats or caps, sunglasses, drinking water in reusable bottles, comfortable walking shoes, and portable power banks.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: When will the main air show take place in Varanasi?

The grand public aerial display of "Wings Over Ganges" will take place on Sunday, October 25, 2026, from 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Full practice sessions will be held on October 22 and 24 between 10:30 AM and 1:30 PM.

Q2: Where is the main focal venue for spectators?

Namo Ghat (near Rajghat in Northern Varanasi) serves as the primary demonstration venue and central control center. However, the flight corridor stretches right across the Ganga riverfront towards Dashashwamedh Ghat.

Q3: Do I need to buy tickets or register online?

No tickets or passes are required. The entire show is free for all citizens and pilgrims. Please avoid scam portals claiming to sell VIP passes.

Q4: Are personal camera drones permitted to film the show?

Strictly NO. The airspace over Varanasi is notified as an active military flight Red Zone. Flying personal drones is illegal and subject to immediate police action and confiscation under Indian aviation laws.
