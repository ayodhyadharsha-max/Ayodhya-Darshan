Chitrakoot Dham — Complete Spiritual & Sacred Exile Guide

Complete spiritual & travel guide to Chitrakoot Dham. Learn about Kamadgiri Parikrama, Ramghat Aarti, Hanuman Dhara, Sati Anusuya & Gupt Godavari.

Chitrakoot, the hill of many wonders, is where Bhagwan Shri Ram, Mata Sita, and Shri Lakshman spent 11.5 years of their 14-year forest exile (Vanvas).

Whether you are seeking spiritual peace, ancient temple darshan, or sacred river dip, this guide provides complete information on divine sights and heritage.

1. Kamadgiri Parikrama

The original holy hill of Chitrakoot, Kamadgiri is believed to fulfill all holy desires. Devotees perform a 5 km barefoot Parikrama (circumambulation) around the lush green hill lined with temples.

2. Ramghat & Mandakini Aarti

Set along the banks of the serene Mandakini River, Ramghat is where Saint Tulsidas had the divine vision (darshan) of Lord Ram and Lakshman. Evening Mandakini Aarti is a peaceful experience.

3. Hanuman Dhara & Sphatik Shila

Perched atop a cliff, Hanuman Dhara features a natural mountain stream cascading onto a statue of Lord Hanuman. Nearby Sphatik Shila bears the foot impressions of Shri Ram and Mata Sita.

4. Sati Anusuya Ashram & Gupt Godavari

Located in dense forest, Sati Anusuya Ashram is where Maharishi Atri and Sati Anusuya meditated. Gupt Godavari features twin natural cave streams flowing inside limestone rocks.
