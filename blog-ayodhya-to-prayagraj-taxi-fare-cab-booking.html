2. Route Map & Sightseeing En-Route

The journey follows NH330 passing via Sultanpur and Pratapgarh straight into Prayagraj city. The road is well-paved 4-lane national highway with clean vegetarian family dhabas and fuel stops.

Top Places Covered in Prayagraj Pilgrimage:

  • Triveni Sangam: Holy confluence of Ganga, Yamuna, and Saraswati rivers for sacred bath & boat rides.
  • Bade Hanuman Mandir: Unique reclining idol of Lord Hanuman ji situated near Sangam.
  • Alopi Devi Shakti Peeth: Ancient wooden doli shrine revered as one of the 51 sacred Shaktipeeths.
  • Anand Bhawan & Swaraj Bhawan: Historic heritage estate of the Nehru family.
