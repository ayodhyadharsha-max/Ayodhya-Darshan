Saryu Ghat Aarti Timings Ayodhya: Morning & Evening Schedule (2026)

Published by Ayodhya Dharshan Editorial Team | Updated October 2026

Official Timings: Evening Saryu Maha Aarti begins at 6:30 PM (Summer) and 6:00 PM (Winter) at Naya Ghat / Ram Ki Paidi. Morning Aarti takes place at 5:30 AM.

1. Saryu Maha Aarti Timings Schedule

Attending the divine Saryu Maha Aarti at Naya Ghat on the banks of the sacred Saryu River is an essential experience for every pilgrim visiting Ayodhya Dham. The evening ritual features grand brass lamps, Vedic chanting, bell ringing, and thousands of floating diyas on the river.

Aarti Event Summer Timings (Mar – Oct) Winter Timings (Nov – Feb) Location
Morning Prabhat Aarti 5:30 AM – 6:00 AM 6:00 AM – 6:30 AM Naya Ghat Main Platform
Evening Sandhya Maha Aarti 6:30 PM – 7:15 PM 6:00 PM – 6:45 PM Naya Ghat & Ram Ki Paidi
Light & Sound Show 7:30 PM – 8:15 PM 7:00 PM – 7:45 PM Ram Ki Paidi Water Screen

2. Best Viewing Spots: Naya Ghat vs Ram Ki Paidi

  • Naya Ghat (Main Aarti Platform): This is where the head priests perform the grand 108-wick lamp Aarti. Arrive by 5:45 PM to get front-row seating on the stone steps.
  • Ram Ki Paidi: Located right next to Naya Ghat, featuring stunning lit fountains, laser projections, and peaceful walking promenades.
  • Boat View Saryu Aarti: Renting a decorated wooden boat allows you to watch the vibrant flames of Aarti reflected on the river waters away from the crowds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What time should I reach Saryu Ghat for evening Aarti?

We recommend reaching Naya Ghat at least 30 to 45 minutes before the Aarti time (around 5:45 PM) to get comfortable seating on the steps.

Q2: How much does a boat ride cost during Saryu Aarti?

Hand-rowed wooden boats cost between ₹200 and ₹500 per head depending on group size and duration during evening Aarti hours.
