8 Days · 7 Nights

Full Ramayana Circuit Tour

The ultimate pilgrimage across Northern India. A soul-stirring 8-day journey covering Ayodhya, Naimisharanya, Prayagraj, Chitrakoot, Varanasi, and Vindhyachal Shaktipeeth with full-managed logistics and premium services.

8-Day Itinerary

Day 1 — Welcome to Ayodhya

Arrival & Saryu River Aarti

On arrival in Ayodhya, check in to your hotel. Spend your evening at Ram ki Paidi to witness the divine Saryu River Aarti at sunset.

Day 2 — Ayodhya Temple Darshan

Ram Janmabhoomi & Hanuman Garhi

Perform early morning darshan at Hanuman Garhi and the magnificent Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir. In the afternoon, visit Kanak Bhawan and Dashrath Mahal.

Day 3 — Naimisharanya Excursion

Chakra Tirth & Maa Lalita Devi Shaktipeeth

Embark on a day excursion to Naimisharanya (approx. 2.5 hours). Take a holy bath at Chakra Tirth, seek blessings at Lalita Devi Temple, and visit Vyas Gaddi. Return to Ayodhya for the night.

Day 4 — Transfer to Prayagraj

Triveni Sangam Boat Snan & Cabs

Check out and drive to Prayagraj (approx. 3.5 hours). Board a private boat to Triveni Sangam for a sacred dip. Visit Bade Hanuman Temple, Akshayavat, and Alopi Devi Temple before checking in at your Prayagraj hotel.

Day 5 — Chitrakoot Yatra & Varanasi Drive

Exile Sites & Transfer to Kashi

Drive to Chitrakoot (approx. 2.5 hours). Visit Ramghat on Mandakini River, Kamadgiri Parikrama, Sphatik Shila, and Gupt Godavari caves. In the evening, drive to Varanasi (approx. 4.5 hours) to check in to your hotel.

Day 6 — Varanasi Darshan & Ganga Aarti

Kashi Vishwanath Corridor & Boat Ride

Seek blessings at Kashi Vishwanath temple, Annapurna Mandir, and Kal Bhairav temple. In the evening, watch the majestic Ganga Aarti from a private boat at Dashashwamedh Ghat.

Day 7 — Vindhyachal Devi Dham Excursion

Maa Vindhyavasini Temple (Shaktipeeth)

Perform a day excursion to Vindhyachal Shaktipeeth (approx. 2 hours from Varanasi). Visit Maa Vindhyavasini temple, Ashtabhuja, and Kalikhoh. Return to Varanasi for overnight stay.

Day 8 — Sunrise Boat Ride & Departure

Subah-e-Banaras & Sarnath

Witness a beautiful sunrise boat ride on the Ganga. Visit Sarnath (where Lord Buddha preached his first sermon) before transferring to Varanasi airport or station for departure.

FAQ — Full Ramayana Circuit

How is the itinerary paced?+
The pacing is planned carefully to balance spiritual devotion with physical comfort. We ensure adequate rest times, comfortable private drives, and prioritize ground-level stays or hotels with elevators for ease.
Are entry tickets for Shaktipeeths included?+
Standard and VIP darshan assistance is included in the package. Priority entry coordinates directly with local shrine pandits for seamless entries at Vindhyachal and Naimisharanya.
