2. How We Eliminate Walking Fatigue for Elderly Pilgrims

Visiting Ram Mandir normally requires 2 to 3 kilometers of walking through security corridors, locker areas, and temple paths. For seniors with knee osteoarthritis, back pain, or heart conditions, this can be exhausting. Here is our seamless elder-care system:

🛺 Step 1: Doorstep Cab to Nearest Barrier

Our drivers have authorized commercial permits that take your parents right to the nearest police drop barrier, eliminating 1.5 km of unnecessary walking from distant parking lots.

🔋 Step 2: Battery Golf Cart to Janmabhoomi Path

From the drop barrier, elderly pilgrims board authorized electric golf carts that transport them directly across the outer security ring to the Pilgrim Facilitation Center (PFC).

♿ Step 3: Free Wheelchair & Attendant Escort

At the PFC, our coordinator arranges a sanitized wheelchair with a polite attendant who wheels them through the dedicated senior citizen queue directly to the Garbhagriha for Ram Lalla darshan.

3. The Relaxed 3-Day Senior Citizen Itinerary (Zero Rush)

Unlike standard rushed packages, this itinerary leaves generous 2-hour afternoon rest breaks and spaces temple visits to ensure your parents feel energized throughout:

Day 1: Gentle Arrival, Lift Hotel Check-in & Evening Saryu Aarti

  • Pickup from Ayodhya Airport (AYX) or Railway Station in an AC sedan/Ertiga with smooth driving assistance.
  • Check-in at pre-verified elevator-equipped hotel. Leisurely hot sattvic lunch and 2-3 hours of afternoon rest.
  • 05:30 PM: Drive to Naya Ghat / Ram Ki Paidi. Special seating arrangements arranged for watching the divine Saryu Maha Aarti without standing in crowd pushes. Overnight stay.

Day 2: Morning Ram Lalla Darshan with Wheelchair, Kanak Bhawan & Rest

  • 08:30 AM: Post-breakfast drive to Janmabhoomi Path via golf cart. Seamless wheelchair entry to Ram Janmabhoomi sanctum sanctorum. Spend peaceful moments before Lord Ram Lalla.
  • 11:30 AM: Accessible visit to Kanak Bhawan (ground-level seating).
  • 12:30 PM – 03:30 PM: Return to hotel for pure sattvic lunch, medication schedule, and comfortable afternoon rest.
  • 04:30 PM: Visit Hanuman Garhi (palanquin/doli service arranged for parents unable to climb 76 steps). Overnight stay.

Day 3: Peaceful Guptar Ghat Morning, Souvenir Blessings & Departure

  • 08:30 AM: Relaxed morning drive along the scenic riverside promenade at Guptar Ghat. Clean paved ramps and peaceful river breeze.
  • 11:00 AM: Purchase authentic Ayodhya Ram Mandir prasad boxes and Ramnami shawls.
  • Afternoon: Dedicated cab drop-off at Airport or Railway Station with luggage handling assistance.
