3. Recommended Itineraries: 1, 2, and 3 Days

Depending on your travel schedule, here are the most efficient day-by-day itineraries included in our packages:

Most Popular

2 Days / 1 Night Classic Ayodhya Tour Package Itinerary

  • Day 1 (Morning & Afternoon): Arrival at Ayodhya Dham Junction / Airport. Our chauffeur receives you with luggage assistance and transfers you to your hotel. After freshening up, proceed to the holy Hanuman Garhi Temple for traditional initial blessings, followed by visits to Kanak Bhawan (Sita Ram palace) and Dashrath Mahal.
  • Day 1 (Evening): Visit Ram Ki Paidi for the spectacular Saryu Maha Aarti. Enjoy a peaceful wooden boat ride across the glowing ghats, followed by dinner and overnight stay at your hotel.
  • Day 2 (Morning): Early morning holy bath at Saryu River (optional). Proceed via Janmabhoomi Path for Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir Darshan. Witness the divine Ram Lalla idol, admire the temple's pink sandstone nagara architecture, and collect authentic Trust Besan Laddoo Prasad.
  • Day 2 (Afternoon): Visit Guptar Ghat (the sacred place where Lord Rama took Jal Samadhi), browse local Ayodhya souvenir markets for wooden Ram Mandir replicas and Saryu Jal, followed by transfer to the airport or railway station for departure.

Q2: What is included in an Ayodhya tour package?

Our packages include 3-star AC hotel accommodation, daily pure vegetarian breakfast, private sanitised AC cabs (Swift Dzire or Ertiga) for airport/station pickup and temple sightseeing, Ram Mandir queue guidance, and evening Saryu Aarti boat rides.

Q3: How many days are ideal for an Ayodhya pilgrimage?
