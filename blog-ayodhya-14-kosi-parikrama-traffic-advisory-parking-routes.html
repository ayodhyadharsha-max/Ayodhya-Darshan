Ayodhya 14 Kosi & 5 Kosi Parikrama 2026 Traffic Advisory: Road Diversions, 16 Parking Zones & Cab Guide

📅 Updated: November 16, 2026 ⏱️ 8 min official travel advisory 📍 Ayodhya Dham, Uttar Pradesh

With over 30 to 35 Lakh devotees converging on Ayodhya for the sacred 14 Kosi Parikrama (45 km) and 5 Kosi Parikrama (15 km) during Kartik Mela 2026, the district administration and traffic police have implemented comprehensive road diversions and sealed the circular parikrama corridors. Here is the verified official advisory on route restrictions, 16 satellite parking lots, EV shuttle routes, and booking an authorized Ayodhya tour package.

⚡ Direct Answer: Key Traffic Rules for Ayodhya Parikrama 2026 (Nov 17 - 21)

Vehicle Ban Effective: From Tuesday, November 17, 8:00 PM to Saturday, November 21, 10:00 PM.
Zero-Vehicle Corridors: The entire 45-km 14 Kosi outer circular route and 15-km 5 Kosi inner circular route are declared 100% pedestrian-only zones. No personal four-wheelers or two-wheelers are allowed inside.
Satellite Parking: 16 outer parking lots have been established at entry highways (Naka Bypass, Dahiyawan, Saket College, Naya Ghat Underpass, ITI Ground, and Bharat Kund). From there, devotees can walk or take authorized electric shuttle carts.

2. Intercity Highway Diversion Plan (NH-27 & NH-330)

If you are transiting past Ayodhya without intending to perform Parikrama, follow these mandatory diversion corridors to avoid massive multi-hour bottlenecks:

🚛 Heavy Commercial Vehicles (Trucks, Tankers, Multi-Axle Trailers)

Completely banned from entering NH-27 between Barabanki and Basti. Diverted via Purvanchal Expressway (Sultanpur – Azamgarh – Gorakhpur link) or via Jarwal Road – Colonelganj – Gonda – Utraula – Dumariyaganj – Basti.

🚘 Light Private Cars & Tourist Buses Transiting Through

Allowed on the elevated NH-27 flyover only. All exit ramps into Ayodhya city (Naya Ghat, Saket, Tedhi Bazar, and Mohobara) are sealed by armed police pickets. Vehicles attempting to exit will be turned back to satellite parking.

🚕 Pre-Booked Tour Cabs with Verified Hotel Vouchers

Tourist cabs holding verified hotel reservations inside Ayodhya Dham are granted passage up to designated outer drop barriers (Saket Petrol Pump, Dahiyawan, or Guptar Ghat), where hotel luggage porters and electric buggies assist pilgrims.

4. Reaching Ram Mandir During Parikrama (Senior Citizens & Families)

Many devotees assume Ram Mandir is closed during Parikrama. Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir remains completely open for darshan. However, reaching the temple requires careful planning:

♿ Senior Citizen & Wheelchair Guidance:

  • Private cars cannot drop devotees at the temple gate. Ensure you arrive via an authorized local cab drop point at Saket or Dahiyawan.
  • From the drop point, take authorized government electric shuttle carts directly to Janmabhoomi Path.
  • Free wheelchairs and dedicated attendants are available at the Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra Pilgrim Facilitation Center (PFC).
  • To avoid parikrama foot crowd collisions, the best darshan window is between 11:30 AM and 03:00 PM, when walking parikrama pilgrims rest at roadside bhandaras.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can we enter Ayodhya by cab from Lucknow Airport during Parikrama?

Yes. Cabs from Lucknow Airport (LKO) can travel via NH-27 to the outskirts of Ayodhya. At the Naka Bypass barrier, the driver shows your hotel reservation and navigates via the authorized bypass route to drop you at the designated hotel transit point.

Are auto-rickshaws and e-rickshaws running inside Ayodhya during Parikrama?

Regular private e-rickshaws are restricted from the active 45-km and 15-km parikrama paths to prevent crowd stampedes. However, government-authorized electric shuttle buggies operate on designated peripheral transit corridors between satellite parking lots and Naya Ghat / Ram Mandir.

Are emergency medical facilities and water points available along the route?

Yes. The administration has deployed 32 temporary medical health posts, 45 mobile ambulances with life support, 120 RO drinking water tankers, and over 200 public bio-toilets every 500 meters along the 14 Kosi and 5 Kosi Parikrama routes.
