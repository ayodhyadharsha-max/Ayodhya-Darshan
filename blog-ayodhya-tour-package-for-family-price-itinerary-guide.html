1. Why Ayodhya is India's #1 Family Pilgrimage in 2026-2027

Traveling with three generations under one roof—grandparents, parents, and growing children—is one of the most fulfilling experiences in Indian family life. Following the historic opening of the grand Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir, Ayodhya has transformed into a world-class pilgrimage destination that combines profound spiritual sanctity with modern, convenient civic infrastructure.

Unlike older pilgrimage circuits that involved steep hill climbs or narrow, congested alleyways, modern Ayodhya offers wide pedestrian boulevards (Ram Path, Bhakti Path, and Dharma Path), clean battery-operated golf carts, well-maintained public restrooms, international-standard airports (AYJ), and multi-lane expressways from Lucknow and Gorakhpur. Children get to learn the timeless values of the Ramayana firsthand, while grandparents fulfill their lifelong devotion to Prabhu Shri Ram in total comfort.

4. Recommended 3 Days / 2 Nights Relaxed Family Itinerary

Our family itinerary is specially structured so that neither children get cranky nor elderly parents feel rushed:

📅 Day 1: Warm Welcome, Hotel Check-in, Hanuman Garhi & Saryu Aarti

• Morning pickup from Lucknow Airport or Ayodhya Railway Station in your sanitized private AC cab.
• Check-in to your family rooms; enjoy fresh vegetarian lunch and brief rest.
• 03:30 PM: Visit Shri Hanuman Garhi Mandir (76 steps with handrails, or assistance for senior citizens). Seek blessings of Ayodhya Kotwal Lord Hanuman.
• 05:30 PM: Head to Ram ki Paidi & Naya Ghat. Attend the divine Saryu Maha Aarti at 6:30 PM with thousands of floating earthen lamps.
• 07:45 PM: Watch the magnificent Sound, Light & Laser show at Ram Katha Park.
• Return to hotel for hot sattvic family dinner; overnight stay.

🛕 Day 2: Grand Ram Janmabhoomi Darshan, Kanak Bhawan & Guptar Ghat

• 07:30 AM: Healthy family breakfast at hotel.
• 08:30 AM: Proceed to Bhavya Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir. Experience the mesmerizing darshan of Shri Ram Lalla, Ram Darbar, and Kuber Tila with timed queue coordination.
• 11:30 AM: Visit Kanak Bhawan (golden palace of Sita-Ram) and Dashrath Mahal.
• 01:30 PM: Family lunch break; afternoon rest during temple closure hours.
• 04:30 PM: Sunset drive to historic Guptar Ghat on the banks of Saryu River where Lord Ram took Jal Samadhi. Enjoy a peaceful motorized boat ride for the whole family.
• Evening shopping for local souvenirs, Ayodhya peda prasad, and holy Ramcharitmanas.

🌸 Day 3: Surya Kund, Mani Parbat & Departure

• 08:30 AM: Relaxed breakfast at hotel.
• 10:00 AM: Visit the beautifully restored Surya Kund and the sacred Mani Parbat.
• 12:30 PM: Hotel checkout and departure drop to Ayodhya Airport / Railway Station or return transfer to Lucknow with cherished family memories and the eternal blessings of Prabhu Shri Ram.
