📍 Best Places to Visit in Chitrakoot (Sightseeing List)

  • Ramghat & Mandakini Aarti: Take a sacred boat ride on Mandakini river and witness evening floating lamp Aarti.
  • Kamadgiri Parikrama & Kamtanath Mandir: Perform the 5 km holy parikrama around the wish-fulfilling Kamadgiri hill.
  • Gupt Godavari Caves: Explore natural limestone pair caves with underground flowing Mandakini stream.
  • Hanuman Dhara Waterfall & Ropeway: Ascend the hilltop shrine via modern ropeway or stairs where natural spring cools Lord Hanuman.
  • Sphatik Shila & Janki Kund: Visit the rock bearing Lord Ram & Sita Ji's footprints along Mandakini.
  • Sati Anusuya Ashram & Ganesh Bagh: Sacred forest ashram of Maharshi Atri & Mata Anusuya, and historic Ganesh Bagh palace.

📅 Detailed 1-Day & 2-Day Chitrakoot Tour Itinerary

07:00 AM

Pickup & Arrival at Sacred Ramghat

Pick up from Chitrakoot Dham Karwi Station (CKTD), Satna (STA), or Prayagraj. Begin with holy snan at Ramghat on Mandakini river.

08:30 AM

Kamadgiri Parikrama & Temple Visit

Embark on the 5 km holy Kamadgiri Parikrama path, visiting Kamtanath Temple and surrounding ancient shrines.

11:30 AM

Gupt Godavari Caves Exploration

Visit the natural pair of caves where underground Mandakini stream flows silently through carved rock formations.

02:00 PM

Lunch & Sphatik Shila Visit

Enjoy traditional local lunch, followed by visit to Sphatik Shila where Lord Ram & Sita Ji sat together.

04:30 PM

Hanuman Dhara Ropeway & Sita Rasoi

06:30 PM

Grand Mandakini Aarti at Ramghat

Witness evening illumination and floating oil lamp Aarti along Ramghat before transfer to station/hotel.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Chitrakoot Yatra)

Where is Chitrakoot located and how to reach?

Chitrakoot is located on the UP-MP border, ~130 km from Prayagraj. The nearest railway station is Chitrakoot Dham Karwi (CKTD) or Satna (STA).

Is Kamadgiri Parikrama accessible for senior citizens?

Yes, the 5 km Parikrama pathway is fully paved, shaded, and battery e-rickshaws or palanquins (doli) are available for elderly pilgrims.

What is the distance from Prayagraj to Chitrakoot?

Prayagraj to Chitrakoot distance is approximately 130 km (approx. 3 hours drive via AC cab).
