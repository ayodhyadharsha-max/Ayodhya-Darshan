Kashi Vishwanath VIP Sugam Darshan Guide 2026:
Online Ticket Booking, Aarti Rates & Gate Entry

Visiting the holy city of Kashi? Here is the complete 2026 guide for Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple VIP Sugam Darshan tickets, online pass availability, Aarti rates, dress codes, gate access, and private AC taxi charges.

📌 Quick Summary: Kashi Vishwanath VIP Pass 2026

  • Sugam Darshan Ticket Rate: ₹300 per person (Skip long queues & direct entry corridor).
  • Mangala Aarti Ticket Rate: ₹500 per person (03:00 AM - 04:00 AM sacred ritual).
  • Main Entry Gates: Gate 4 (Godaulia Gate) & Ganga River Corridor Gate (Lalita Ghat).
  • Dress Code: Dhoti-Kurta / Saree mandatory for Sparsh Darshan; Formal Indian / Casuals allowed for Sugam line.

🛕 What is Kashi Vishwanath Sugam Darshan?

Kashi Vishwanath Dham Corridor in Varanasi attracts millions of devotees daily. Standard general queues during peak hours and weekends can take 2 to 4 hours. To make pilgrimage convenient for families, senior citizens, and travelers with tight schedules, the Temple Trust offers Sugam Darshan (VIP Pass).

With a Sugam Darshan pass, pilgrims enter through dedicated fast-track corridors, receive Prasad, and get close darshan of the sacred Golden Jyotirlinga within 20 to 30 minutes.

📱 How to Book VIP Sugam Darshan Passes Online

  1. Visit the official Kashi Vishwanath Temple Trust portal or request pass assistance via Ayodhya Dharshan guided yatra packages.
  2. Select Sugam Darshan / Aarti Pass and choose your preferred date and time slot.
  3. Upload a valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID).
  4. Complete online payment and download your digital QR barcode ticket onto your smartphone.
  5. Report at Gate 4 or the Corridor Desk 15 minutes before your allotted slot with physical ID proof.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Kashi VIP Darshan)

What is the ticket rate for Kashi Vishwanath VIP Darshan?

The standard VIP Sugam Darshan pass costs ₹300 per devotee. Mangala Aarti passes cost ₹500.

Are mobile phones allowed inside Kashi Vishwanath Corridor?

Electronic items, mobile phones, leather belts, and bags are prohibited inside the inner sanctum. Free locker counters are provided at Gate 4 and Corridor entrances.
