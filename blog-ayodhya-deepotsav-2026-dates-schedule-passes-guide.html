Ayodhya Deepotsav 2026: Dates, Full Event Schedule & VIP Guide

Published by Ayodhya Dharshan Editorial Team | Updated October 12, 2026

Grand Festival Alert: Ayodhya Deepotsav 2026 marks the magnificent lighting of over 25 Lakh earthen lamps (Diyas) along the 55 ghats of Saryu River and Ram Ki Paidi, accompanied by aerial drone shows, international Ramlila performances, and 3D laser projections.

2. How to Secure Passes & Smooth Travel During Deepotsav

Due to heavy crowds of over 10 to 15 lakh pilgrims arriving in Ayodhya during Deepotsav week, strict vehicle movement protocols and pedestrian corridors are established by local authorities. Here is what you need to know:

  • Vehicle Restrictions: Outer ring road checkpoints permit only registered tourist vehicles and pre-booked hotel tourist cabs. Booking in advance with verified agencies like Ayodhya Dharshan ensures seamless police checkpoint clearance.
  • Hotel & Tent City Availability: Hotels near Ram Mandir and the Saryu Tent City sell out weeks prior. Early advance reservations are highly recommended.
  • Guided Ghat View Points: Our private local guides assist senior citizens and family groups to peaceful viewing zones away from stampede choke points.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Can general tourists enter Ram Mandir on Deepotsav day?

Yes, temple darshan remains open from 6:30 AM to 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM to 10:00 PM. Expect longer queues; pre-registering Sugam Darshan passes is strongly advised.

Q2: How early should we reach Ram Ki Paidi for the Diya lighting?

Ghat barricading starts by 3:00 PM. Arrive at designated public entry gates by 3:30 PM to secure prime viewing spots along the steps.
