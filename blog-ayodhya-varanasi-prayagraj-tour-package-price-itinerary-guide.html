2. Comprehensive 5-Day / 4-Night Holy Trinity Itinerary

This itinerary is carefully engineered to eliminate back-and-forth travel, respect temple pooja timings, and allow senior citizens to rest between highway stretches:

Day 1: Arrival in Ayodhya, Ram Janmabhoomi Darshan & Saryu Evening Aarti

  • Morning: Pickup from Maharishi Valmiki Airport Ayodhya (AYX) or Ayodhya Dham Railway Station (AY). AC cab transfer to hotel.
  • Afternoon: Visit the sanctum sanctorum of Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra for divine darshan of Ram Lalla. Visit Hanuman Garhi (climb 76 steps to seek Bajrangbali’s permission).
  • Evening: Witness the majestic evening Saryu Maha Aarti at Naya Ghat / Ram Ki Paidi illuminated with thousands of lamps. Overnight stay in Ayodhya.

Day 2: Kanak Bhawan, Guptar Ghat & Scenic Drive to Prayagraj (165 km)

  • 07:30 AM – 10:00 AM: Morning visit to Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal, and the serene Guptar Ghat on River Saryu.
  • 10:30 AM – 02:30 PM: Depart for Prayagraj via NH-330 (approx. 3.5 to 4 hours smooth highway drive). Scenic lunch halt near Sultanpur.
  • 03:30 PM – 06:30 PM: Hotel check-in at Prayagraj. Evening visit to Chandra Shekhar Azad Park (Company Bagh) and the historic Anand Bhavan (ancestral home of the Nehru family). Overnight stay in Prayagraj.

Day 3: Triveni Sangam Holy Snan, Bade Hanuman Ji & Drive to Varanasi (125 km)

  • 06:00 AM – 09:00 AM: Board a private motor boat from Sangam Ghat to the sacred confluence of Ganga, Yamuna, and mythical Saraswati. Perform holy Snan, Sankalp, and feed migratory Siberian birds.
  • 09:30 AM – 11:30 AM: Darshan at the rare reclining Bade Hanuman Ji Mandir (Lete Hue Hanuman) and Alopi Devi Shaktipeeth.
  • 12:00 PM – 03:00 PM: Depart for Varanasi via NH-19 (approx. 2.5 hours). Hotel check-in in Varanasi.
  • 05:30 PM – 07:30 PM: Private boat ride at Dashashwamedh Ghat to witness the world-renowned Ganga Maha Aarti from the water. Overnight stay in Varanasi.

Day 4: Shri Kashi Vishwanath Sugam Darshan, Annapurna & Sacred Temples

  • 07:00 AM – 10:00 AM: VIP Sugam Darshan of Shri Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga via Gate No. 4 of the grand Vishwanath Corridor. Darshan of Maa Annapurna and Vishalakshi Devi.
  • 11:00 AM – 01:30 PM: Darshan at Kaal Bhairav Mandir (Kotwal of Kashi) and historic Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple.
  • 03:30 PM – 06:30 PM: Visit Durga Kund Mandir, Tulsi Manas Mandir, and the magnificent marble New Vishwanath Temple (BHU). Overnight stay in Varanasi.

Day 5: Assi Ghat Subah-e-Banaras, Sarnath Excursion & Departure

  • 05:30 AM – 07:30 AM: Witness morning sunrise boat tour from Assi Ghat to Manikarnika Ghat with Vedic chanting and classical music at Subah-e-Banaras.
  • 09:30 AM – 01:00 PM: Excursion to Sarnath (10 km). Visit Dhamek Stupa, Mulagandha Kuti Vihar, Deer Park, and the Archaeological Museum (Lion Capital of Ashoka).
  • 02:30 PM onwards: Transfer to Varanasi Airport (VNS) or Cantt Railway Station (BSB) for your onward journey with divine memories.

Understanding the geographic triangle helps you appreciate why this route is so comfortable when managed with an experienced local driver:

🛣️ Ayodhya to Prayagraj

Distance: 165 km | Time: 3.5 to 4 Hours

🛣️ Prayagraj to Varanasi

Distance: 125 km | Time: 2.5 Hours
Route: Via NH-19 (Grand Trunk Road / 6-lane express corridor) passing Handia and Gopiganj. Widest and fastest stretch of the yatra.

🛣️ Varanasi to Ayodhya (Direct)

Distance: 200 km | Time: 4 to 4.5 Hours
Route: Via NH-135A and Purvanchal Expressway Link or via Jaunpur & Shahganj. Smooth connectivity for round-trip travelers.

4. VIP Darshan, Puja & Boat Coordination Explained

Navigating temple protocols across three different pilgrimage cities can be daunting. Here is how our yatra coordinators assist you at every shrine:

🚩 In Ayodhya: Ram Mandir VIP & Senior Citizen Access

We assist with slot confirmations for Mangala, Shringar, and Sandhya Aartis. For senior citizens, we arrange battery-operated golf carts from the parking hub to Janmabhoomi Path, locker deposit assistance, and wheelchair escorts upon request.

🌊 In Prayagraj: Private Sangam Boat & Snan Safety

We reserve authorized wooden motor boats fitted with standard life jackets. The boat anchors right at the Triveni Sangam platform where devotees can step onto floating wooden decks to take a safe holy dip without wading into deep river currents.

🕉️ In Varanasi: Kashi Vishwanath Sugam Darshan & Evening Aarti Boat

We coordinate ₹300 Sugam Darshan tokens through Corridor Gate No. 4, cutting wait times from 3 hours to 25 minutes. In the evening, a dedicated boat takes you directly in front of Dashashwamedh Ghat with reserved seating for the Maha Aarti.
