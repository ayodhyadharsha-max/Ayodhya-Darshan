Ayodhya 14 Kosi & 5 Kosi Parikrama 2026: Dates, Muhurat, Route & Guide

Published by Ayodhya Dharshan Editorial Team | Updated October 24, 2026

  • 14 Kosi Parikrama (45 km): November 18 – 19, 2026 (Begins on Kartik Akshaya Navami / Amla Navami)
  • 5 Kosi Parikrama (15 km): November 20 – 21, 2026 (Observed on Devuthani / Prabodhini Ekadashi)

  • Starting Point: Naya Ghat / Saryu River (both circuits begin after holy snan)

Every year during the sacred Hindu month of Kartik, the holy city of Ayodhya transforms into a sea of devotion. More than 50 lakh pilgrims gather to perform the ancient circumambulation rituals known as the 14 Kosi Parikrama (चौदह कोसी परिक्रमा) and 5 Kosi Parikrama (पंचकोसी परिक्रमा).

Walking these sacred circuits barefoot is believed to cleanse a devotee of sins accumulated across multiple lifetimes and grant the supreme spiritual merit (punya) of circumambulating all the deities residing in Lord Rama's realm. With the newly built four-lane illuminated Parikrama Marg and upgraded amenities following the inauguration of the Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir, the 2026 Parikrama promises to be more spiritually uplifting than ever.

2. The 14 Kosi Parikrama Route Map & Stopping Spots (45 Km)

The 14 Kosi Parikrama encircles the outer geographical and spiritual boundary of greater Ayodhya Dham. Devotees walk through rural groves, historic ashrams, and riverside ghats:

🚶 Sequential Route Points (45 Km Loop):

  1. Starting Point: Naya Ghat: Devotees take a holy bath in River Saryu, worship Mother Saryu, and start the parikrama amidst chants of "Siyavar Ram Chandra Ki Jai".
  2. Karsevakpuram & Halakara Purva: Moving through the eastern residential sector and green countryside.
  3. Mauni Baba Ashram & Darshan Nagar: Major resting area with continuous community bhandaras (free food stalls), tea, and lemon water kiosks.
  4. Surya Kund: The ancient sacred water body where King Dasharatha worshipped the Sun God. A major midnight rest point.
  5. Naka Hanuman Garhi: The southern sentinel post of Lord Hanuman guarding the entry to Ayodhya.
  6. Guptar Ghat: The world-renowned peaceful ghat on the Saryu where Lord Rama performed Jal Samadhi to return to Vaikuntha.
  7. Rajghat & Ram Ki Paidi: Entering back into northern Ayodhya through Lakshman Ghat.
  8. Finish Line: Naya Ghat: Concluding the 45 km circuit with a final prayer and dip in the sacred river.

3. The 5 Kosi Parikrama Route Map & Sacred Shrines (15 Km)

Held on Devuthani Ekadashi, the 5 Kosi Parikrama encircles the sanctified inner core where Lord Rama spent his childhood:

👣 Sequential Route Points (15 Km Loop):

  1. Saryu Snan at Prahlad Ghat / Naya Ghat: Traditional start after dawn.
  2. Udiya Tiraha & Tedhi Bazar: Walking through the festive decorated lanes of historic Ayodhya.
  3. Mani Parvat & Sugriv Parvat: Ancient sacred mounds offering a panoramic view of the temple city.
  4. Janmabhoomi Path & Ram Mandir Periphery: Circling the holy sanctum of Shri Ram Janmabhoomi.
  5. Hanuman Garhi & Kanak Bhawan: Devotees offer bowed respects from the path.
  6. Nageshwarnath Temple: The ancient Shiva temple established by Lord Rama's son, Kush.
  7. Lata Mangeshkar Chowk & Ram Ki Paidi: Completing the circuit with evening deep daan.

4. Essential Practical Tips for Parikrama Devotees

  • Footwear Considerations: While traditional pilgrims walk barefoot (नंगे पैर), you may carry lightweight cotton socks or soft walking footwear if you have sensitive soles or diabetes.
  • Traffic Diversions: During the Parikrama days, regular vehicular movement is strictly banned inside the parikrama boundary. All private cars and cabs must be parked at authorized satellite parking lots (Chauka Ghat, Tedhi Bazar multi-level parking, etc.).
  • Bhandara & Refreshments: Hundreds of charitable trusts set up 24x7 free langars serving hot khichdi, tea, seasonal fruits, and clean drinking water along every 500 meters of the path.
  • Luggage & Valuables: Carry only essentials in a light waist pouch. Leave heavy suitcases and laptops safely in your hotel room.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: When will the 14 Kosi Parikrama take place in Ayodhya in 2026?

The 14 Kosi Parikrama will be held on November 18 to 19, 2026 (Kartik Akshaya Navami). It begins with a holy bath at Naya Ghat on the banks of the Saryu River.

Q2: When is the 5 Kosi Parikrama in 2026?

The 5 Kosi Parikrama is scheduled for November 20 to 21, 2026, marking Devuthani (Prabodhini) Ekadashi, encircling the inner 15 km holy core of Ayodhya.

Q3: Is medical and food assistance available on the Parikrama Marg?

Yes! Over 500 free bhandaras, lemon water kiosks, and first-aid camps operated by local seva samitis and the Ayodhya District Administration operate around the clock along both the 45 km and 15 km routes.

Q4: Can senior citizens participate in the Parikrama?

Senior citizens who cannot walk the 45 km 14 Kosi loop often choose the 15 km 5 Kosi Parikrama, or hire authorized electric golf carts on Janmabhoomi Path for comfortable darshan.
