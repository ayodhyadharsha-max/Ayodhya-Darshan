🛕 Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir Darshan & Aarti Timings 2026

Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir welcomes thousands of pilgrims daily for sacred darshan of Ram Lalla Virajman. To ensure a peaceful and hassle-free darshan experience, the Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra Trust operates structured morning and evening darshan slots along with special Aarti passes.

🕒 Daily Darshan & Aarti Timings Schedule

  • Morning Darshan Slot: 06:00 AM to 12:00 PM
  • Afternoon Temple Closure: 12:00 PM to 02:00 PM (Bhog & Shringaar)
  • Evening Darshan Slot: 02:00 PM to 10:00 PM
  • Mangala Aarti: 06:30 AM (Requires advance booking pass)
  • Shringaar Aarti: 12:00 PM (Requires advance booking pass)
  • Sandhya Aarti: 07:30 PM (Requires advance booking pass)

🎫 How to Book Ram Mandir VIP Sugam Darshan Passes

Pilgrims can book Sugam Darshan (VIP Entry) and Aarti passes online via the official website of Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra. For elderly citizens, families, and high-density rush periods, Ayodhya Dharshan provides full local assistance with escorted entry, wheelchair support, and private vehicle drops to entry gates.

  • ID Proof Requirement: Carry original Aadhaar Card or Passport matching the pass name.
  • Locker & Cloakroom: Mobile phones, electronic items, leather belts, and bags must be deposited at official free cloakrooms before entering the security queue.
  • Escorted Assistance: Our local Ayodhya travel team guides you directly through the designated gate queues to save hours during peak festive rush.

📍 Must-Visit Sacred Places in Ayodhya

🚩 Hanuman Garhi

Ancient hilltop temple dedicated to Lord Hanuman. According to custom, pilgrims visit Hanuman Garhi first to take blessings before Ram Janmabhoomi darshan.

👑 Kanak Bhawan

Beautiful palace gifted to Mata Sita by Queen Kaikeyi upon her wedding with Lord Ram. Houses exquisite idols of Shri Ram and Sita Ji adorned in gold crowns.

🌊 Saryu River Aarti & Ram Ki Paidi

Witness the mesmerizing evening Grand Saryu Aarti at Ram Ki Paidi where floating diyas light up the sacred river banks as Vedic chants fill the air.

🔱 Nageshwarnath Temple

Ancient Shiva temple established by Lord Ram's son Kusha, located near Ram Ki Paidi ghats.

🍲 Local Ayodhya Food & Sattvic Dining Guide

Ayodhya offers pure vegetarian (Sattvic) culinary delights without onion or garlic. Must-try traditional treats include hot Rabri-Jalebi at Hanuman Garhi lane, Ghee Aloo-Kachori, Awadhi Bati-Chokha, and fresh Mathura Pedas. Clean, hygienic bhojanalayas are available close to Ram Path and Naya Ghat.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Ayodhya Yatra)

What are the Ram Mandir Ayodhya darshan timings?

Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir is open daily from 06:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 02:00 PM to 10:00 PM. Mangala Aarti is at 06:30 AM, Shringaar Aarti at 12:00 PM, and Sandhya Aarti at 07:30 PM.

How to book Ram Mandir VIP Darshan passes online?

Sugam Darshan passes can be booked on the official Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra website or assisted directly through Ayodhya Dharshan tour packages.

How many days are enough for Ayodhya trip?

A 2-Day or 3-Day tour itinerary is ideal to cover all holy temples, enjoy evening Saryu River Aarti, take a boat ride, and explore local Ayodhya markets comfortably.
