Naimisharanya (Neemsar) — Complete Spiritual Tapobhoomi Guide

Complete spiritual & travel guide to Naimisharanya (Neemsar). Learn about Chakra Tirth, Vyas Gaddi, Lalita Devi Shaktipeeth & 88000 rishis tapobhoomi.

Naimisharanya, located on the banks of Gomti River in Sitapur district, is the sacred land where 88,000 rishis performed penance and Maharishi Ved Vyas compiled the 18 Mahapuranas.

Whether you are seeking spiritual peace, ancient temple darshan, or sacred river dip, this guide provides complete information on divine sights and heritage.

1. Chakra Tirth (Holy Water Tank)

According to Puranic history, Lord Brahma released His divine Manomaya Chakra to mark the center of the universe. The place where the wheel ring submerged became the holy circular tank known as Chakra Tirth.

2. Vyas Gaddi (Vyas Peeth)

Under an ancient 5000-year-old banyan tree, Maharishi Ved Vyas divided the Vedas into four branches and narrated the Srimad Bhagavatam & 18 Puranas to disciple Maharishi Suta.

3. Lalita Devi Shaktipeeth

One of the prominent Shaktipeeths of Uttar Pradesh, Lalita Devi Temple is dedicated to Goddess Lalita Tripurasundari, worshipped for health, prosperity, and divine strength.

4. Hanuman Garhi (Dakshinamukhi)

Shrine dedicated to Lord Hanuman after his return from Patal Lok bringing Ahiravan victory. The large south-facing stone idol is worshipped with vermillion (sindoor).
