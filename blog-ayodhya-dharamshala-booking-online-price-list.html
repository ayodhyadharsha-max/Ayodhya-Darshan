2. How Dharamshala Allotment & Booking Works in Ayodhya

Many first-time visitors expect instant instant one-click online confirmations like commercial hotel aggregators. However, trust-managed properties follow specific rules:

  • Original Government ID Required: Every staying guest (including family members) must present a physical original Aadhaar card or government ID during check-in.
  • Strict Check-in & Check-out Timings: Most dharamshalas enforce an 8:00 AM to 10:00 AM check-out cycle to allow room sanitization for incoming pilgrims.
  • Sattvic Atmosphere: Smoking, alcohol, and non-vegetarian consumption are strictly banned within and around dharamshala premises.
  • Heavy Festival Demand: During peak festival windows (Deepotsav, Ram Navami, Kartik Purnima), dharamshalas fill up months ahead.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Which dharamshala is closest to Shri Ram Janmabhoomi?

Kanak Bhawan Dharmarth Trust and Gujarati Dharamshala in Ram Kot are the closest, located within an 8-minute walking distance of Ram Mandir Gate 1.

Q2: Are food / bhojanalaya facilities available in Ayodhya dharamshalas?

Yes, most major dharamshalas operate pure vegetarian bhojanalayas serving wholesome thali meals between ₹60 and ₹120 per head.
