The opening of the grand Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir has made Ayodhya the most sought-after pilgrimage in India. An Ayodhya Darshan tour package takes the stress out of the trip — darshan, stays, travel and timings all handled — so your family can simply focus on the divine.

This guide covers how many days you need, a sample itinerary, what a package costs, and how to arrange smooth (and VIP) darshan.

How many days do you need?

Most families find 2 nights / 3 days ideal for a focused Ayodhya darshan. It gives you unhurried time at the Ram Mandir, the major temples, and an evening at the Saryu without rushing. If you want to add Prayagraj or Varanasi, plan 4–6 days.

Sample 3-day Ayodhya itinerary

  • Day 1 — Arrival, hotel check-in, evening Saryu Aarti at Ram ki Paidi.
  • Day 2 — Early Ram Janmabhoomi darshan, Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan, Nageshwarnath and Dashrath Mahal.
  • Day 3 — Sunrise snan at the Saryu, optional Guptar Ghat, departure.

What to see in Ayodhya

  • Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir — the main darshan, the heart of the yatra.
  • Hanuman Garhi — the hilltop temple of Lord Hanuman, traditionally visited first.
  • Kanak Bhawan — the palace gifted to Mata Sita, known for its beautiful idols.
  • Ram ki Paidi & Saryu Aarti — the riverfront steps and the lamp-lit evening aarti.

Ayodhya tour package cost

A typical 3-day Ayodhya darshan tour package starts around ₹8,900 per person and varies with hotel category, group size and season. Most packages include:

  • Accommodation (hotel or clean dharamshala) close to the temples
  • All local travel by private AC vehicle, with pickup and drop
  • A knowledgeable guide and darshan assistance
Tip: Travelling as a group of 6–10 brings the per-person cost down significantly, since vehicle and guide costs are shared.

VIP & senior-friendly darshan

Where the temple administration offers sugam / suvidha darshan, we arrange priority entry, aarti seating and wheelchair assistance — a big relief for elderly pilgrims during peak days.

Best time to visit

October to March offers the most pleasant weather. Ram Navami (spring) and Deepotsav (Diwali) are spectacular but very crowded — book early. See our full guide on the best time to visit Ayodhya.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are enough for Ayodhya darshan?

2 nights / 3 days is comfortable for the Ram Mandir and all major temples. Add days if combining with Varanasi or Prayagraj.

Does the package include VIP Ram Mandir darshan?

We arrange priority/sugam darshan wherever it is officially available, along with senior and wheelchair assistance.

What is the cost of an Ayodhya tour package?

From about ₹8,900 per person for 3 days, depending on hotel category, group size and season.
