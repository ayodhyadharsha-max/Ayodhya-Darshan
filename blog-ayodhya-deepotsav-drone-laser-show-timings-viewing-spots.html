Ayodhya Deepotsav 2026 Drone & Laser Show: Timings, Free Spots & Passes

Published by Ayodhya Dharshan Editorial Team | Updated October 28, 2026

⚡ Quick Snapshot: Deepotsav 2026 Drone & Laser Spectacle

  • Main Event Date: Saturday, November 7, 2026 (Diwali Eve / Choti Diwali)
  • Mega Drone Show Time: 7:15 PM – 8:00 PM (2,000+ synchronized aerial drones over River Saryu)
  • 3D Holographic Laser Show: 8:00 PM – 9:30 PM (Projected on Ram Ki Paidi water mist screen)
  • Diya Lighting World Record: 5:45 PM – 7:00 PM (Targeting 28 to 33 Lakh earthen lamps across 55 ghats)
  • Public Ticket / Pass: 100% FREE (No passes required for sky-viewing from open ghats)

The 10th milestone edition of Ayodhya Deepotsav 2026 is set to illuminate the holy city like never before. While the grand record-breaking lighting of over 28 to 33 lakh earthen lamps (diyas) along the sacred Saryu riverbanks remains the spiritual soul of the festival, the high-tech Mega Drone Light Show and 3D Holographic Laser Projection have become the most eagerly anticipated attractions for visiting pilgrims and international tourists.

If you are planning to witness this historic spectacle in Ayodhya on Saturday, November 7, 2026, here is your definitive, on-ground spectator guide covering exact flight schedules, reality of Ram Ki Paidi entry passes, top free viewing spots, traffic diversions, and verified Ayodhya tour package options.

1. What to Expect: 2,000+ Aerial Drones & 3D Laser Storytelling

The 2026 aerial display will feature India's largest synchronized drone fleet soaring 300 to 500 feet directly above the waters of River Saryu and Ram Ki Paidi. Operating in seamless coordination with a dynamic symphonic musical score, the drones will form magnificent luminous figures across the night sky:

🏹 Epic Ramayana Formations in the Sky

Witness glowing 3D formations of Lord Rama breaking Shiva's Dhanush at Janakpur, Veer Hanuman carrying the Sanjeevani Parvat, and the triumphant return on the Pushpak Viman to Ayodhya Dham.

🛕 Grand Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir

The climax of the drone show depicts the majestic silhouette of the newly consecrated Ram Mandir, complete with fluttering saffron Dhwaja, radiating tricolour laser beams across Ayodhya's skyline.

🌊 Water Mist Holographic Projections

Simultaneously, high-power laser projectors will cast 3D holographic animations across the flowing water fountains of Ram Ki Paidi, narrating chaupais from Goswami Tulsidas's Ramcharitmanas.

🎇 Green Eco-Friendly Aerial Fireworks

Concluding the performance, low-smoke, zero-pollution aerial fireworks will burst into vibrant hues of gold, saffron, and crimson, reflecting off the illuminated waters of Saryu.

3. The Reality of Ram Ki Paidi Passes: Beware of Ticket Scams

⚠️ Urgent Warning: No Public VIP Passes are Sold Online

Every year, thousands of unsuspecting tourists fall prey to fake websites, social media pages, and agents claiming to sell "VIP Ram Ki Paidi Passes" for ₹500 to ₹2,500. The Ayodhya District Police and UP Tourism have reiterated that no physical or digital passes are sold to the public. Access to the immediate ghat steps on November 7 is strictly restricted to Guinness World Record volunteers (wearing official barcode badges), accredited media, and state guests. Any person claiming to sell you entry passes is committing a cybercrime.

4. Top 5 Free Public Viewing Spots to Watch the Drone & Laser Show

Because the drones fly several hundred feet in the air, you do not need to be on the steps of Ram Ki Paidi to enjoy an awe-inspiring, crystal-clear view. Here are the top 5 recommended locations:

  1. Chaudhary Charan Singh Ghat & Lakshman Ghat:
    Located immediately adjacent to Ram Ki Paidi along the continuous riverside promenade. The open sky above the river offers a direct, unhindered angle of the 2,000-drone formations and ambient laser reflections. Reach by 4:30 PM to secure prime railing spots.
  2. The Eastern Sand Beds (Reti across River Saryu):
    For professional photographers and families looking to avoid crushing crowds, taking an authorized registered boat across to the eastern sandy riverbed before 3:00 PM is the ultimate secret spot. It offers a majestic wide-angle panoramic view of both the floating drones and the entire illuminated ghatline.
  3. Naya Ghat Promenade & Lata Mangeshkar Chowk:
    The grand Veer Savarkar Setu / Naya Ghat bridge walkway provides an elevated vantage point overlooking the river basin. The laser projections and drone transitions are vividly visible against the temple spires.
  4. Ram Katha Park Open Amphitheatre:
    Equipped with colossal 4K LED screens and concert-grade surround sound systems, Ram Katha Park allows thousands of families to sit comfortably on lush lawns and experience the entire broadcast with live multicam drone angles.
  5. Guptar Ghat Riverfront Promenade:
    Situated 8 km upstream, Guptar Ghat hosts its own serene diya-lighting ceremonies with live LED streaming of the main Ram Ki Paidi drone show, making it ideal for elderly travelers avoiding high-density zones.

5. Traffic Advisories, Parking & Cab Entry Rules for November 7

Due to an estimated footfall of over 25 lakh visitors, the Ayodhya Police Commissionerate enforces strict vehicle restrictions:

  • Vehicle Red Zone: Dharma Path, Ram Path, and the Naya Ghat corridor are closed to non-permit private cars from 8:00 AM on November 7 until midnight.
  • Satellite Parking Hubs: Outstation private vehicles and taxis arriving via Lucknow NH27 or Gorakhpur must park at authorized multi-level facilities at Tedhi Bazar, Saket Mahavidyalaya ground, or Chauka Ghat.
  • E-Rickshaws & Golf Carts: Authorized electric shuttle rickshaws operate continuously from satellite parking areas to peripheral drop points near Naya Ghat for pilgrims.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: When is the Ayodhya Deepotsav Drone Show and Laser Show in 2026?

The grand Drone Show will take place on Saturday, November 7, 2026, from 7:15 PM to 8:00 PM over the Saryu River and Ram Ki Paidi, followed by recurring 3D laser projection shows until 9:30 PM.

Q2: Is viewing the Drone Show and Laser Show free for the public?

Yes, 100% free. The drone show takes place high in the open sky, and anyone standing on adjacent ghats, bridges, or the opposite riverbed can view the entire show without paying any admission fee.

Q3: Why are general tourists restricted from the steps of Ram Ki Paidi on Nov 7?

The steps are occupied by thousands of designated university volunteers actively managing the Guinness World Record grid of 28+ lakh diyas and oil tanks. To prevent stampedes and damage to the record setup, physical access to the steps is strictly regulated by police.

Q4: How can I reach Ayodhya during Deepotsav from Lucknow or Gorakhpur?

You can book private sanitised AC cabs with authorized route permits through Ayodhya Dharshan. Direct doorstep pickup is available from Lucknow Airport/Junction (from ₹2,199) and Gorakhpur (from ₹2,499).

Q5: What should pilgrims carry while watching the evening shows?

Carry a light jacket or shawl (early November evenings by the Saryu River turn pleasant to cool), reusable water bottles, power banks for your phone cameras, and avoid carrying heavy bags or prohibited electronic items near temple checkpoints.
