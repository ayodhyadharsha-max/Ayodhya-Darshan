Day-by-Day 7-Day Tour Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Varanasi (Kashi) — Evening Ganga Aarti Boat Ride

Arrive at Varanasi Airport (VNS) or Varanasi Junction Railway Station (BSB). Our private AC driver picks you up and transfers you to your hotel. Relax before heading to Dashashwamedh Ghat for the legendary evening Ganga Aarti. Enjoy a private wooden boat ride to witness the grand chants and flickering oil lamps along the illuminated ghats.

Day 2: Kashi Vishwanath VIP Darshan, Kaal Bhairav & Sarnath

Early morning VIP Darshan at Kashi Vishwanath Temple (Golden Temple of Shiva) and Mata Annapurna Temple. Visit the protective deity of Varanasi at Kaal Bhairav Temple, followed by Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple and Sarnath (where Lord Buddha preached his first sermon).

Day 3: Varanasi to Prayagraj (Sangam Snan & Hanuman Temple) — Drive to Ayodhya

Drive 2.5 hours to Prayagraj. Take a holy boat ride to the sacred confluence of Ganga, Yamuna, and Saraswati (Triveni Sangam). Visit the famous Bade Hanuman Ji Temple (Leta Hua Hanuman), Anand Bhavan, and Alopi Devi Shaktipeeth. In the afternoon, proceed for a 3.5-hour smooth highway drive to Ayodhya Dham.

Day 4: Grand Ayodhya Ram Mandir Darshan, Hanumangarhi & Saryu Aarti

Experience unforgettable darshan at the magnificent Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir. Visit Hanuman Garhi (where Lord Hanuman sits as Ayodhya's guardian), Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal, and Sugriva Bhawan. In the evening, attend the divine Saryu River Maha Aarti at Ram Ki Paidi with breathtaking laser light illumination.

Day 5: Ayodhya Sightseeing — Travel to Mathura / Vrindavan

Visit Surya Kund, Nageshwarnath Temple, and Chhoti Devkali Temple in the morning. After a sattvic lunch, board an express train or private highway vehicle to Mathura & Vrindavan (the land of Shri Krishna).

Day 6: Vrindavan Banke Bihari, Prem Mandir & Nidhivan

Morning darshan at the world-famous Shri Banke Bihari Temple in Vrindavan. Visit ISKCON Vrindavan (Krishna Balaram Mandir) and Nidhivan. In the evening, witness the mesmerising light show and marble architecture at Prem Mandir.

Day 7: Mathura Krishna Janmabhoomi, Gokul & Departure

Visit Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi Temple Complex in Mathura, Dwarkadhish Temple, and Vishram Ghat on the Yamuna banks. Option to visit Gokul & Govardhan Parikrama before transferring to Delhi / Agra / Mathura Junction for your return journey.
