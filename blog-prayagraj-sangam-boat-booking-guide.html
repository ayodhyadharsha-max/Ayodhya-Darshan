Rates, Holy Dip Rituals & Local Sightseeing

📌 Quick Summary: Triveni Sangam Boat Rates 2026

  • Shared Boat Ticket: ₹100 – ₹150 per seat.
  • Private Hand-Rowed Boat: ₹800 – ₹1,500 per boat (1 to 6 persons).
  • Motorboat Hire: ₹1,500 – ₹2,500 per boat (Fast trip to Sangam point).
  • Lete Hanuman Temple Timings: 05:00 AM – 02:00 PM & 05:00 PM – 10:00 PM.

🌊 The Sacred Significance of Triveni Sangam

Triveni Sangam in Prayagraj is the holy confluence of three sacred rivers — Ganga, Yamuna, and the invisible Saraswati. Devotees from across the globe take a holy dip (Snan) at the Sangam point to cleanse sins and achieve spiritual liberation.

The distinct muddy yellow color of the Ganga merging with the deep blue waters of the Yamuna is a mesmerizing divine sight that can be experienced only by taking a boat ride to the central Sangam wooden platforms.

🛕 Must-Visit Attractions in Prayagraj

  1. Lete Hanuman Temple (Bade Hanumanji): Unique lying idol of Lord Hanuman located right near Sangam Ghat.
  2. Alopi Devi Temple: Reclaimed Shakti Peeth where no idol is worshipped, only a wooden doli.
  3. Anand Bhawan & Swaraj Bhawan: Ancestral home of the Nehru family turned heritage museum.
  4. Akshayavat & Patalpuri Temple: Sacred immortal banyan tree inside the Prayagraj Fort complex.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Prayagraj Yatra)

What is the distance between Ayodhya and Prayagraj?

The road distance between Ayodhya and Prayagraj is approximately 168 km and takes about 3.5 to 4 hours via private AC taxi.

Is it safe for senior citizens to take a boat dip at Sangam?

Yes, wooden bath platforms with railings, changing rooms, and life jackets are provided at the Sangam confluence point.
