Sugam Passes, Aarti Slots & Entry Rules

📌 Quick Summary: Ram Mandir VIP Pass Rules 2026

  • Sugam Darshan Slot: Fast-track queue-less entry within 20–30 minutes.
  • Aarti Passes (Free): Mangala Aarti (06:30 AM), Shringaar Aarti (12:00 PM), Sandhya Aarti (07:30 PM).
  • Entry Gate: Sugam Pass Holders report at Janmabhoomi Path Gate 3.
  • ID Requirement: Original Aadhaar Card / Passport mandatory.

🚩 Why You Need a VIP Sugam Pass in 2026

With over 1.5 lakh devotees visiting Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir daily, general queues during weekends, festivals, and sawan months can take 2 to 4 hours.

By booking a VIP Sugam Darshan Pass, pilgrims enter through dedicated air-conditioned corridors, receive sanctified Ram Lalla Prasad, and get close darshan of Ram Lalla within 20 minutes.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Ram Mandir VIP Pass)

Is Ram Mandir VIP Sugam Darshan pass free?

Official Aarti passes are free on the trust portal. Sugam passes have nominal processing slots or can be bundled into guided tour packages with escorts.

What mobile items are allowed inside Ram Mandir?

Mobile phones, leather belts, and earphones are strictly banned. Free lockers are provided at PFC 1 & PFC 2 counters on Janmabhoomi Path.
