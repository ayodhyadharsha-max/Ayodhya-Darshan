🛕 Shri Ram Mandir Ayodhya Darshan Guide & Booking Information

Standing majestically at the sacred Shri Ram Janmabhoomi, the grand Ram Mandir is the spiritual heart of Ayodhya Dham. Millions of devotees visit daily for divine darshan of Ram Lalla Sarkar.

⏱️ Darshan Timings Morning: 6:00 AM – 12:00 PM
Evening: 2:00 PM – 10:00 PM

Ram Mandir Entry Rules & Documents: All pilgrims must carry a valid original Government ID (Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID). Mobile phones, smartwatches, belts, wallets, and leather items are restricted inside temple premises. Free locker facility is available at the entry complex. 📍 Best Places to Visit in Ayodhya (Top Sightseeing Attractions)

1. Hanuman Garhi Temple Ayodhya

It is custom to seek blessings at Lord Hanuman's hilltop fort before visiting Ram Mandir. Climb 76 steps to reach the shrine of Hanuman ji guarding Ayodhya Dham.

2. Kanak Bhawan Temple

The ornate palace gifted to Mata Sita by Queen Kaikeyi upon her marriage. Marvel at the golden crowns and beautiful idols of Lord Ram and Sita Ji.

3. Saryu River, Ram Ki Paidi & Evening Aarti

Take a sacred dip in Saryu River, enjoy motorboat rides, and witness the grand evening Saryu Aarti illuminated by thousands of floating lamps at Ram ki Paidi.

4. Nageshwarnath Temple & Dashrath Bhawan

Visit Nageshwarnath Mandir (established by Lord Ram's son Kusha) and Dashrath Bhawan, the ancestral palace of King Dashrath.

5. Guptar Ghat, Mani Parvat & Surya Kund

Explore Guptar Ghat on the Saryu banks where Lord Ram took Jal Samadhi, visit historic Mani Parvat, and rejuvenate at the newly restored Surya Kund lake complex.

🚗 How to Reach Ayodhya (Flight, Train & Road Transport)

  • By Flight: Maharishi Valmiki International Airport Ayodhya (AYJ) has direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Kolkata & Hyderabad. (15 km to Ram Mandir).
  • By Train: Ayodhya Dham Junction (AY) and Ayodhya Cantt (AYC) connect to major Indian cities via Vande Bharat, Amrit Bharat, and Express trains.
  • By Road: Excellent 4-lane highway connectivity from Lucknow (130 km), Varanasi (200 km), Prayagraj (160 km), and Gorakhpur (135 km). Private AC cabs & taxis available.
