Ayodhya Ram Mandir Senior Citizen & Wheelchair Guide (2026)

Published by Ayodhya Dharshan Editorial Team | Updated October 18, 2026

Stress-Free Yatra for Parents: Bringing your parents or grandparents to seek the divine blessings of Balak Ram? You do not need to worry about long walking stretches. The temple infrastructure features free automated wheelchairs, electric golf carts, dedicated ramps, and passenger lifts to ensure a comfortable darshan.

1. Free Wheelchair & Volunteer Attendant Facility

The Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra Trust operates an extensive fleet of wheelchairs at the Pilgrim Facilitation Centre (PFC) located on Janmabhoomi Path:

Facility / Service Cost / Fee How to Avail
Standard Wheelchair 100% Free Show Senior Citizen ID (Aadhaar Card) at PFC Counter
Electric Battery Golf Carts Free Service Board directly at Janmabhoomi Path entrance corridor
Trust Volunteer Escort Complimentary Assists pilgrims who do not have an able-bodied family member
Sanctum Passenger Lifts Free Access Available at the northern and southern plinth ramps

2. Step-by-Step Route for Elderly Devotees

Follow this optimal route to minimize walking distance and ensure maximum physical comfort:

  • Step 1 (Doorstep Drop by Cab): Have your private cab drop your family directly at the closest authorized drop-point on Ram Path near Birla Dharamshala or Janmabhoomi Path.
  • Step 2 (Battery Golf Cart Ride): Board the electric shuttle golf-cart that transports elderly passengers smoothly across the 800-meter paved Janmabhoomi Path.
  • Step 3 (Wheelchair & Locker Center): Deposit all mobile phones, leather belts, and footwear at the special senior citizen counter inside the Pilgrim Facilitation Centre (PFC). Collect your assigned wheelchair.
  • Step 4 (Dedicated Ramp & Lift Access): Wheelchair users bypass the 32 main entrance stairs and proceed through the gentle, non-skid ramp directly to the sanctum viewing hall (Gudh Mandap).
  • Step 5 (Resting Pavilions & First Aid): Shaded resting halls with chilled RO drinking water and 24/7 medical first-aid doctors are available within the PFC courtyard.

3. Pro-Tips for Visiting Hanuman Garhi with Elderly Parents

While Ram Mandir is fully wheelchair-accessible with ramps and elevators, Hanuman Garhi requires climbing 76 steep stone stairs:

  • Palki / Doli Service: For senior citizens who cannot climb stairs, traditional manual Doli (palanquin) services are available at the base of Hanuman Garhi for a fixed nominal rate.
  • Best Timing: Plan Hanuman Garhi early in the morning (6:30 AM to 8:00 AM) or late afternoon (4:00 PM) to avoid afternoon heat and large crowds.
  • Kanak Bhawan Access: Kanak Bhawan has ground-level courtyard access with only 4–5 small steps, making it very comfortable for senior devotees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Can we bring our own personal wheelchair from home?

Yes, personal non-motorized foldable wheelchairs are allowed inside the temple premises after passing through the security screening scanner.

Q2: How much time does senior citizen darshan take?

With wheelchair and Sugam access, darshan typically takes between 45 to 60 minutes, compared to 2.5 to 3.5 hours in the regular general queue.

Q3: Are medical facilities available inside the mandir complex?

Yes, the Trust provides a well-equipped medical dispensary with emergency oxygen, doctors, stretchers, and cardiac emergency teams at the Pilgrim Facilitation Centre.

Q4: Is there an age requirement for senior citizen priority entry?

Devotees aged 60 years and above, as well as individuals with medically certified mobility issues, qualify for priority wheelchair and golf-cart assistance. Carrying an Aadhaar card is recommended.
