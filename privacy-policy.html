Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 04, 2026

Ayodhya Dharshan ("we", "our", "us") values your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you book tour packages, cabs, or hotel stays with us.

Information We Collect

We collect details provided by you when filling inquiry forms or contacting us via WhatsApp/Phone, such as name, phone number, travel dates, and passenger count for yatra booking arrangements.

Use of Information

Your details are strictly used for coordinating cab pick-and-drop, hotel reservations, and temple entry permits. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.

Google, as a third-party advertising vendor, uses cookies to serve advertisements on Ayodhya Dharshan. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.

Editorial Integrity & Sourcing

Every yatra guide and travel itinerary published on Ayodhya Dharshan is authored by 100% local pilgrimage escorts and verified against official Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra and Kashi Vishwanath Temple Trust advisories.
