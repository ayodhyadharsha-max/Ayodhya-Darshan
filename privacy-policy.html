Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026 | Ayodhya Dharshan Teerth Yatra

At Ayodhya Dharshan (accessible from https://www.ayodhyadharshan.com/ and https://ayodhyadharshan.com/), the privacy of our devotees, pilgrims, and website visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information that is collected and recorded by Ayodhya Dharshan and how we utilize, protect, and handle it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information directly when you voluntarily provide it while inquiring about a pilgrimage yatra, booking a tour package, scheduling a cab, or reaching out via phone, email, or WhatsApp. This information may include:

  • Contact Information: Full name, mobile/WhatsApp telephone number, and email address.
  • Yatra Booking Details: Travel dates, number of pilgrims (including senior citizens and children), chosen circuits (e.g., Ayodhya, Kashi Vishwanath, Prayagraj Sangam), pickup location, and specific accommodation preferences.
  • Government Identification (When Required): Photo identity proof details strictly required for temple darshan passes or hotel check-in compliance per local authority regulations.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used exclusively for legitimate pilgrimage operations and customer seva:

  • To coordinate AC taxi pickup and transfers, hotel reservations, and temple visit schedules.
  • To communicate booking confirmations, driver details, yatra itineraries, and emergency advisories.
  • To provide 24×7 on-ground assistance and answer devotee queries.
  • To maintain accounting records and comply with applicable statutory requirements.

We never sell, rent, trade, or share your personal contact details with third-party telemarketers or external advertising networks.

3. Log Files & Standard Web Analytics

Like most standard websites, Ayodhya Dharshan uses log files. These files merely log visitors to the site — usually a standard procedure for hosting companies and part of hosting services' analytics. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. This information is not linked to any information that is personally identifiable and is used solely for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking user movement, and gathering demographic information to enhance user experience.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

Google is an authorized third-party advertising vendor on our website. Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve advertisements to visitors based on their visit to ayodhyadharshan.com and other websites on the Internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at www.aboutads.info.

5. Cookie Policy & Browser Controls

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found at the browsers' respective websites (such as Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, or Microsoft Edge).

6. Data Security & Confidentiality

We implement robust physical, technical, and administrative security measures to protect the personal information submitted to us, both during transmission and once received. All pilgrimage coordination files are handled with strict confidentiality by verified local personnel.

7. Editorial Integrity & Temple Information Accuracy

Every travel guide, temple timing, route distance, and yatra itinerary published on Ayodhya Dharshan is researched by local pilgrimage coordinators and verified against official advisories issued by the Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra Trust, Shri Kashi Vishwanath Special Area Development Board, and Uttar Pradesh Tourism authorities.

8. Children's Privacy

Protecting the privacy of young children is especially important. Ayodhya Dharshan does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that our website has in its database the personal information of a child under 13, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

9. User Rights & Grievance Redressal

In accordance with applicable data protection regulations (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your records once your pilgrimage journey has concluded.
