5 Days · 4 Nights

Ayodhya Prayagraj Varanasi Tour

Embark on the classic trinity yatra of Northern India. Seek blessings at Lord Ram's birth site in Ayodhya, take a sacred dip at the Triveni Sangam in Prayagraj, and witness the eternal beauty of Kashi Vishwanath in Varanasi.

5-Day Itinerary

Day 1 — Welcome to Ayodhya

Ayodhya Arrival & Saryu Aarti

Arrival at Ayodhya airport or station, transfer to your hotel. Visit Ram ki Paidi for the sunset Saryu River Aarti.

Day 2 — Ayodhya Core Darshan

Hanuman Garhi & Ram Janmabhoomi

Perform early morning darshan at Hanuman Garhi and the grand Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir. In the afternoon, visit Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal, and local highlights.

Day 3 — Prayagraj Sangam Snan

Triveni Sangam & Bade Hanuman

Drive to Prayagraj (approx. 3.5 hours). Take a private boat to the Triveni Sangam for a holy dip. Visit Bade Hanuman Temple, Akshayavat, and Alopi Devi Temple before checking in at your Prayagraj hotel.

Day 4 — Transfer to Varanasi & Ganga Aarti

Kashi Arrival & Evening Aarti

Check out and drive to Varanasi (approx. 2.5 hours). Explore the Kashi Vishwanath Temple corridor and witness the majestic evening Ganga Aarti from a private boat. (Note: Dev Deepawali festival bookings require special arrangements; read our Varanasi Dev Deepawali Guide for booking procedures).

Day 5 — Sunrise boat & Departure

Subah-e-Banaras & Farewell

Enjoy a sunrise boat ride on the Ganga, visit Sarnath, and check out for departure drop-off at Varanasi airport or station.

FAQ — Trinity Yatra

How long is the drive between these cities?+
Ayodhya to Prayagraj is about 170 km (approx. 3.5 hours) and Prayagraj to Varanasi is about 120 km (approx. 2.5 hours) via smooth national highways.
Are priest fees and pooja charges covered?+
Basic guide/escort fees are included. Custom sankalps, rudrabhishek, or pind daan rituals in Prayagraj/Kashi will be coordinated separately with verified local pandas.
