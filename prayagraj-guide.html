Prayagraj (Sangam) — Complete Spiritual & Sacred Sightseeing Guide

Complete spiritual & travel guide to Prayagraj (Allahabad). Learn about Triveni Sangam Snan, Bade Hanuman Ji, Akshayavat, Alopi Devi & Fort.

Prayagraj, the King of Pilgrimages (Teerthraj), sits at the holy confluence of three sacred rivers — Ganga, Yamuna, and the invisible Saraswati.

Whether you are seeking spiritual peace, ancient temple darshan, or sacred river dip, this guide provides complete information on divine sights and heritage.

1. Triveni Sangam (Sacred Confluence)

The holy Triveni Sangam is the ultimate pilgrimage destination where taking a dip (snan) in the confluence waters is believed to cleanse all sins and grant liberation. Wooden boats carry devotees from Sangam Ghat to the deep confluence point.

2. Reclining Bade Hanuman Ji Mandir

Located near Prayagraj Fort, this unique temple enshrines a massive 20-foot reclining idol of Lord Hanuman positioned below ground level. During monsoon, Ganga waters touch the feet of the deity.

3. Akshayavat & Patalpuri Fort Temple

Situated inside the historic Akbar Fort near the riverbank, the immortal banyan tree (Akshayavat) and underground Patalpuri Temple hold immense Vedic significance dating back to ancient Puranic times.

4. Alopi Devi Shaktipeeth & Anand Bhawan

Alopi Devi is a unique Shaktipeeth where no idol is worshipped, but rather a sacred doli (palanquin). Nearby Anand Bhawan offers a glimpse into India freedom movement history.
