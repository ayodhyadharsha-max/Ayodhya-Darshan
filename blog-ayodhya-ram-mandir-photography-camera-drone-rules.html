Ayodhya Ram Mandir Photography, Camera & Drone Rules (2026)

Published by Ayodhya Dharshan Editorial Team | Updated October 20, 2026

Security & Legal Advisory: Every visitor dreams of capturing the majestic grandeur of Ayodhya Ram Mandir. However, due to stringent security protocols enforced by CRPF, UP Police, and the Trust, all cameras, smartphones, and drones are strictly prohibited inside the sanctum complex. Here are the exact official rules and the best alternate photo spots in the city.

1. Camera & Gadget Ban Inside Shri Ram Janmabhoomi

The Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra Trust strictly enforces a zero-device policy across the inner security perimeter:

Equipment Type Inside Ram Mandir Complex Outside Heritage Ghats & Chowks
Smartphones & Mobile Cameras ❌ Strictly Banned (Deposit in PFC Locker) ✅ Allowed Everywhere
DSLR / Mirrorless Cameras ❌ Strictly Prohibited ✅ Allowed at Ghats & Public Corridors
Action Cameras (GoPro / Osmo) ❌ Prohibited at Scanners ✅ Allowed on Saryu Boats & City Paths
Aerial Drones (All Sizes) ❌ High Security Red Zone (Strictly Prohibited) ❌ City-Wide No-Fly Zone (Requires DM Permit)

2. Drone Flying Warning: Strict Red Zone Regulations

Under the civil aviation security guidelines and UP Police directives, Ayodhya Dham has been classified as a High-Security Red Zone:

  • No Unauthorized Drone Flights: Flying recreational nano or micro drones anywhere over Ram Mandir, Janmabhoomi Path, Saryu Ghat, or Ram Ki Paidi is strictly illegal.
  • Confiscation & Legal Action: Anti-drone electromagnetic guns and radar detection are deployed throughout the airspace. Unauthorized drones will be confiscated immediately, and operators face FIR under Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita.
  • Official Media Permits: Only government-registered media houses with written clearance from the District Magistrate and Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) Ayodhya can operate aerial drones during authorized festivals.

3. Top 6 Picture-Perfect Photography Spots in Ayodhya

You can still capture breathtaking, Instagram-worthy memories of your pilgrimage at these legal public viewpoints:

  • 1. Ram Ki Paidi Reflection Promenade: Famous for stunning evening reflections of ancient temples in the serene river water, illuminated by thousands of colorful lights and fountains.
  • 2. Lata Mangeshkar Chowk: Features the magnificent 14-tonne, 40-foot bronze Veena sculpture surrounded by lotus ponds—the most viral selfie point in Ayodhya.
  • 3. Surya Stambh on Dharma Path: 40 towering golden Surya Stambhs (sun pillars) line the grand 4-lane ceremonial highway, creating majestic golden-hour perspectives.
  • 4. Sunset Saryu Boat Cruise: Capture mesmerizing evening hues over the river while traditional brass-lamp Ganga Aarti commences along Naya Ghat.
  • 5. Guptar Ghat Riverfront: A clean, peaceful heritage riverfront lined with classical colonial and Awadhi architecture, marble promenades, and landscaped gardens.
  • 6. Dashrath Mahal Courtyard: Beautiful carved wooden doorways, classical frescoes, and spiritual sadhu portraits in the open courtyard area.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Can I hire a professional photographer for family photos in Ayodhya?

Yes, local professional photographers can be hired for family portraits and traditional photoshoots at public sites such as Ram Ki Paidi, Guptar Ghat, and Surya Stambh.

Q2: Where do I safely deposit my DSLR camera before entering Ram Mandir?

You can deposit cameras and gear at the free Trust computerized lockers at the Pilgrim Facilitation Centre (PFC), or simply leave them safely locked in your private Ayodhya Dharshan cab.

Q3: Is photography permitted during the evening Saryu Aarti?

Yes! Photography and video recording are fully permitted during the grand Saryu Aarti at Ram Ki Paidi and Naya Ghat from both the riverbank steps and private wooden boats.

Q4: Are tripods or gimbals allowed at Ram Ki Paidi?

Handheld mobile gimbals and small travel tripods are generally permitted for personal vlogging in public areas, provided they do not obstruct pilgrim movement or security patrols.
