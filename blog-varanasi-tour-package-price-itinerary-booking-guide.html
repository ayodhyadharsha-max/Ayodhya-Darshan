2. Recommended Day-by-Day Varanasi Tour Itineraries

Navigating Varanasi requires strategic planning because vehicles are restricted near Godowlia and the ghats. Here are our time-tested itineraries designed for maximum spiritual peace and zero fatigue:

🌟 Plan A: 2-Day / 1-Night Classic Kashi Yatra Itinerary

Best for: Weekend getaways, working professionals, and pilgrims arriving via Vande Bharat Express or direct flights.

Day 1: Arrival, Hotel Check-in, Kashi Vishwanath Dham & Ganga Aarti

  • 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM: Pickup from Varanasi Cantt (BSB) / Banaras (BSBS) Railway Station or Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport (VNS). Private AC transfer to hotel.
  • 01:00 PM – 03:00 PM: Fresh up, sattvic lunch, and short rest.
  • 03:30 PM – 05:30 PM: Visit Shri Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga Temple via Corridor Gate No. 4 (Sugam Darshan entry). Darshan of Maa Annapurna Mandir & Vishalakshi Shaktipeeth.
  • 06:00 PM – 07:30 PM: Board pre-booked private boat at Dashashwamedh Ghat for the world-renowned Ganga Maha Aarti from the riverfront.
  • 08:00 PM: Stroll through Godowlia market, taste hot Tamatar Chaat and Banarasi Paan. Overnight stay at hotel.

Day 2: Subah-e-Banaras, Kaal Bhairav, Sarnath & Departure

  • 05:30 AM – 07:30 AM: Early morning sunrise boat ride from Assi Ghat to Manikarnika Ghat. Witness morning Vedic chanting and classical music at Subah-e-Banaras.
  • 08:00 AM – 09:30 AM: Traditional breakfast of hot Kachori-Jalebi and creamy Malaiyo (winter special) near Thatheri Bazar.
  • 10:00 AM – 11:30 AM: Darshan at Kaal Bhairav Mandir (The Kotwal of Kashi; considered essential before leaving Varanasi).
  • 12:00 PM – 03:30 PM: Drive to Sarnath (10 km). Visit Dhamek Stupa, Mulagandha Kuti Vihar, Deer Park, and the Archaeological Museum (Lion Capital of Ashoka).
  • 04:30 PM: Cab drop-off at Airport or Railway Station for your onward journey with divine blessings.

🏛️ Plan B: 3-Day / 2-Night Complete Kashi, Heritage & Spiritual Tour

Best for: Families with senior citizens, leisurely exploring Kashi’s 84 ghats, and silk shopping.

In addition to the Day 1 and Day 2 visits above, Day 3 includes:

  • Morning: Darshan at the historic Sankat Mochan Hanuman Mandir, Durga Kund Mandir, and Tulsi Manas Mandir (where Goswami Tulsidas composed Ramcharitmanas).
  • Mid-day: Explore the sprawling campus of Banaras Hindu University (BHU) and the magnificent marble New Vishwanath Temple (VT).
  • Afternoon: Cross the Malviya Bridge to visit Ramnagar Fort, the 18th-century ancestral palace of the Maharaja of Kashi, housing vintage car and palanquin exhibits.
  • Evening: Visit genuine master weaver centers in Madanpura / Lallapura for authentic GI-tagged handloom Banarasi silk sarees without middlemen commission traps.

3. Kashi Vishwanath VIP & Sugam Darshan Booking Guide 2026

Since the inauguration of the grand Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham Corridor, visiting the Jyotirlinga has become significantly smoother and cleaner. However, during festivals, Mondays, and winter months, general queues can take 2 to 4 hours.

🎫 Sugam Darshan Pass (₹300)

Provides priority entry through dedicated AC waiting lounges directly to the sanctum sanctorum (Garbhagriha). Reduces waiting time to 20-30 minutes.

Available Online & Offline

🕉️ Mangala Aarti Pass (₹500 - ₹1,500)

Held at 3:00 AM every morning. Devotees witness the waking and first holy shringar of Mahadev. Must be booked 15-30 days in advance due to strict limited quotas.

High Demand Booking

👘 Dress Code & Locker Guidelines

To enter the Garbhagriha for Sparsh Darshan (touching the Jyotirlinga), traditional Indian attire (dhoti-kurta for men, saree for women) is mandatory. Free lockers are provided inside Corridor Gate 4.

4. Authentic Banarasi Food & Local Cultural Delicacies

A trip to Kashi is incomplete without experiencing its legendary street gastronomy. All our tour packages feature verified recommendations for 100% pure sattvic vegetarian dining:

🍲 Tamatar Chaat

Served boiling hot in an earthen kulhad with roasted cumin syrup at Kashi Chaat Bhandar, Godowlia.

🥛 Banarasi Malaiyo

Winter delicacy made of whipped milk dew infused with saffron, pistachios, and almonds at Chaukhamba.

Thick hand-churned lassi served near Manikarnika Ghat with dry fruits and seasonal fruit toppings.

🍃 Meetha Banarasi Paan

The authentic Maghai betel leaf with gulkand, saunf, and silver vark at Keshav Tambool Bhandar.

6. Frequently Asked Questions About Varanasi Tour Packages

Is Varanasi safe for senior citizens and elderly pilgrims?

Yes, Varanasi is very welcoming for senior citizens. With the new Kashi Vishwanath Corridor, there are escalators, ramps, and battery-operated golf carts available from the riverfront gate. Our tour packages include dedicated assistance, private cabs to drop you as close to temple gates as permissible, and wheelchair arrangements upon prior request.

How far in advance should I book my Varanasi tour package?

During peak season (October to March) and festive dates like Dev Deepawali and Maha Shivratri, hotels and reliable private boats sell out 3 to 6 weeks in advance. We recommend booking at least 15 to 20 days ahead to secure preferred hotel categories and early Mangala Aarti slots.

What type of vehicle is provided for family groups?

For 2-3 travelers, we provide comfortable AC sedans (Maruti Dzire). For 4-6 travelers, spacious AC Ertiga or Toyota Innova Crysta is assigned. For large joint family groups of 9 to 16 members, we operate luxury 12-seater and 17-seater Tempo Travellers with push-back seats.

Can we also visit Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam) from Varanasi?

Absolutely! Prayagraj is only 125 km (approx. 2.5 hours) from Varanasi via NH-19. We offer an easy day-excursion from Varanasi to Prayagraj to perform holy snan at Triveni Sangam, visit Bade Hanuman Ji Mandir, and return to Varanasi the same evening.
